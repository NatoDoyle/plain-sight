## Sources cited in tactics/affinity-fraud.md

1. SEC Office of Investor Education and Advocacy, "Affinity Fraud" Investor Alert (sec.gov/files/ia_affinityfraud.pdf; Investor.gov) and SEC Division of Enforcement affinity-fraud page — definition (groups preyed upon; fraudster as member; enlisted leaders as unwitting victims); "many affinity frauds are Ponzi or pyramid schemes"; verify the investment *and* the seller regardless of who recommends it (EDGAR, BrokerCheck, IAPD, state regulator); secrecy/guaranteed-return red flags. Regulatory (US; not legal advice).
2. NASAA, "Informed Investor Advisory: Affinity Fraud" — the under-reporting dynamic (victims try to resolve within the group). FINRA Investor Education Foundation — general fraud under-reporting (~11% lost vs ~4% who report; a *general* statistic, not affinity-specific). Regulatory/NGO.
3. Madoff investment scandal — the archetypal affinity Ponzi, run heavily through Jewish community institutions, charities, and country clubs. Figures (cite precisely): **~$64.8B** = fictional value on customer statements; **~$17.5B** = actual principal (SIPC/Picard trustee), the large majority since recovered. Sources: SIPC/Madoff Recovery Initiative; reporting in NYT/AP. Do not cite "$50B" or "$65B" as money lost.
4. Faith-based affinity cases: Baptist Foundation of Arizona (~$590M commonly cited, ~11,000 investors; Crotts & Grabinski convicted 2006; Arthur Andersen settled ~$217M); Greater Ministries International (Gerald Payne; ~$500M from ~18,000; "double your money in 17 months," scripture-laden, bribed church leaders; shut down 1999); Ephren Taylor (SEC complaint ~$11M, 2012; criminal sentencing ~$16M/400+ victims, ~19–20 years; African American megachurches). Attribute figures to source; grade supported.
5. SEC immigrant-community examples (from the SEC affinity-fraud materials): schemes targeting Dominican/Brazilian (and Chinese/Vietnamese/Haitian) communities; an Asian/Latino-targeted cloud-services MLM that raised >$65M. Regulatory.
