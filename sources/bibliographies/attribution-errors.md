# Bibliography log — attribution-errors

Sweep date 2026-08-14 (4 parallel lenses: academic/empirical, skeptic/replication, exploitation/regulatory, recognition/phenomenology). WebSearch budget was exhausted before the sweeps ran, so verification went through the Crossref API, PubMed E-utilities (efetch), EuropePMC, OpenAlex, SAGE full text, Semantic Scholar (rate-limited 429 on two lenses), and Wikipedia citation apparatus as cross-check; APA journals (JPSP, *Psychological Bulletin*, PSPB), Elsevier titles, and the OFT PDF blocked direct fetches. Synthesis 2026-08-15.

Sweep corrections adopted into the draft: **Mezulis depression figure is attenuation to d = 0.21, NOT reversal** (three lenses independently corrected the steer; the "depressive realism reverses it" claim belongs to older selected-sample work) — flagged as a first-class caveat in the file. **Krull et al. 1999 kills the "East Asians don't show the FAE" gloss** ("no cultural difference emerged"); the real cultural variable is responsiveness to situational salience (Choi & Nisbett 1998) plus developmental divergence in spontaneous explanation (Miller 1984). **Many Labs 2 replicated correspondence bias at d = 1.82** with little WEIRD/non-WEIRD heterogeneity — the file's strongest single replication anchor. **Malle 2006 is the humility exhibit**, told straight: overall d ≈ 0, surviving only as a valence effect Malle himself reads as self-serving bias in costume. **Burger 1981's robust core is the similarity moderator**, not the severity main effect (the unconditional "severity raises blame" version replicated inconsistently) — reframed accordingly and made the hinge of the victim-blaming exploitation bullet. **Hewstone 1990 gives only "limited support"** for the ultimate attribution error; Pettigrew's prejudice-level prediction failed; "intergroup attribution bias" named as the accurate label. **Kneer & Machery 2019**: the moral-luck blame asymmetry largely vanishes within-subjects; only punishment is robustly outcome-dependent (Cushman 2008) — moral luck kept short and graded qualified. **System justification split**: phenomena supported, motive contested, with the SIMSA line quoted directly ("we all agree that system justification does occur. The issue is why it occurs?"). **BJW split kept as the defensive payload**: BJW-others is the victim-blaming dimension, BJW-self is largely protective — so the file never tells a survivor to give up believing things can be fair. **Trait ascription, group attribution, and reactive devaluation graded thin in-file and explicitly barred from carrying load.**

Scoping decisions (from the exploitation lens's cross-cutting note, adopted): "astroturfed false consensus" invokes the **false consensus effect**, which is not one of this file's thirteen entries — the file claims only the group-output → member-attitude inference and routes false consensus to [[mind-reading-illusions]] in text; **moral licensing** (Monin & Miller) likewise routed to [[self-evaluation-illusions]]; affinity-fraud exploitation prose stays on the authority/unity edges rather than being forced in here. Mechanism homes for **self-serving bias** (Mezulis) and **just-world** (Lerner & Simmons) move here from `tactics/projection-blame-shifting.md` per the steer; that tactic's prose is untouched and gains an `exploits` edge at the link step. Deliberately **no `safety:` flag** — "why didn't you leave?" is this file's most-quoted phrase, so the Caveats section routes DV-adjacent situations to the safety-flagged [[coercive-control]] and [[dv-safety-planning]] rather than absorbing crisis guidance into a bias file. Red-team review (pipeline step 4) NOT yet run; status `drafted`.
