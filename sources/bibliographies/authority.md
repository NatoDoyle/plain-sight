**Grade assigned:** `supported`, trending `established` within aviation and clinical decision support — with the fence stated plainly in the entry that this is task-performance research on trained operators of *known, imperfect* systems, and that extending it to consumers meeting an AI answer with no error rate attached is **inference, not finding**. That inference is where the manipulation relevance lives and it is labelled as inference.

**Routing:** the entry routes the growth area to [[ai-enabled-manipulation]] — "the system says," "the algorithm flagged your account," "the AI reviewed it" as authority claims with no verifiable person behind them: a title with no holder, a jurisdiction with no edges, and an unusually effective foreclosure of appeal because there is nobody to ask *how do you know?* One counter is added to the file's existing set (out-of-band verification, domain-checking the halo, dual control): **make a human own it** — ask who is putting their name to the decision, on the principle that a recommendation nobody will sign for is an unowned authority claim.
