## Sources cited in dynamics/con-anatomy.md

1. Maurer, D. W. (1940). *The Big Con: The Story of the Confidence Man.* — the canonical big-con stages in con men's own argot (put-up, play, rope, tale, convincer, breakdown, send, touch, blow-off, fix); the "store"; short vs big con; the dishonest-opportunity hook that buys the mark's silence. Ethnographic/linguistic; the uncredited basis for *The Sting*.
2. Konnikova, M. (2016). *The Confidence Game: Why We Fall for It… Every Time.* — the con's progression mapped to the mark's psychology; thesis that we fall because of the story we want to believe, and that intelligence is not protective (can increase vulnerability). Popular synthesis of social/cognitive psychology.
3. Contemporary corroboration: U.S. Secret Service / FBI / state-AG materials on investment fraud and pig-butchering (the early-"withdrawal" convincer; the urgent escalation) — see [[romance-scam-arc]]. Regulatory/investigative.
