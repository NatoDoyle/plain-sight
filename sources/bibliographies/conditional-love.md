## Sources cited in tactics/conditional-love.md

1. Rogers, C. R. (1961). *On Becoming a Person* (and 1959 theory) — unconditional positive regard; "conditions of worth." Foundational humanistic theory.
2. Assor, A., Roth, G., & Deci, E. L. (2004). "The Emotional Costs of Parents' Conditional Regard: A Self-Determination Theory Analysis." *Journal of Personality* 72(1), 47–88 — conditional positive vs. negative regard; both carry emotional costs. Supported.
3. Roth, G., Assor, A., Niemiec, C. P., Ryan, R. M., & Deci, E. L. (2009). "The Emotional and Academic Consequences of Parental Conditional Regard…" *Developmental Psychology* 45(4), 1119–1142 — compares PCPR, PCNR, and autonomy support; **both conditional forms harmful** (introjected regulation, contingent self-esteem, resentment, suppressed emotion), autonomy support beneficial. Meta-analytic support: Haines, J. E., & Schutte, N. S. (2023), "Parental conditional regard: A meta-analysis," *Journal of Adolescence*. Supported.
4. Crocker, J., & Wolfe, C. T. (2001). "Contingencies of Self-Worth," *Psychological Review* 108(3), 593–623 (+ Crocker & Knight, 2005) — contingent self-worth as the downstream effect; Otterpohl et al. (2021) links parental conditional regard → contingent self-esteem. Supported.
