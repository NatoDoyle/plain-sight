## Sources cited in tactics/disinformation-playbooks.md

1. Paul, C., & Matthews, M. (2016). "The Russian 'Firehose of Falsehood' Propaganda Model." RAND Corporation (PE-198) — four features (high-volume/multichannel; rapid/continuous/repetitive; no commitment to objective reality; no commitment to consistency); "don't counter the firehose of falsehood with the squirt gun of truth." Authoritative (model).
2. Wardle, C., & Derakhshan, H. (2017). *Information Disorder* (Council of Europe) — disinformation vs misinformation vs malinformation (intent is the dividing line). Roberts, M. (2018). *Censored* — flooding / reverse censorship. "Flood the zone" (Bannon, reported). Scholarly/documented.
3. Documented actors/research: Russian Internet Research Agency (US v. Internet Research Agency, Mueller indictment, Feb 16 2018); "Doppelganger" cloned-outlet campaign (EU DisinfoLab, 2022); Stanford Internet Observatory; DiResta, R. (2024), *Invisible Rulers*; Oxford Internet Institute computational-propaganda project (Woolley & Howard, *Computational Propaganda*, 2018). Documented.
4. Effects (contested/limited): Grinberg, N., et al. (2019), *Science* 363(6425) — ~1% of users accounted for ~80% of fake-news exposures; Allcott, H., & Gentzkow, M. (2017), *J. Economic Perspectives* 31(2):211–236 — limited reach/persuasion in 2016; Guess, Nyhan & Reifler (2020), *Nature Human Behaviour* — concentrated consumption; Guess, Nagler & Tucker (2019), *Science Advances* eaau4586 — older users shared ~7× more (Facebook). The "backfire effect" has largely failed to replicate. Empirical; effects disputed.
5. Counters: van der Linden, S. (2023), *Foolproof*; Roozenbeek, J., & van der Linden, S. (Bad News game, 2019; *Science Advances*, 2022) — prebunking/inoculation, effects modest and decaying, with a 2024 failed replication on real/fake discrimination. Wineburg, S., & McGrew, S. (2019) — lateral reading. Phillips, W. (2018), *The Oxygen of Amplification*; boyd, d. & Golebiewski, M., "Data Voids" (Data & Society) — don't-amplify. Supported (not settled).
