## Sources cited in tactics/golden-child-scapegoat.md

1. Family-systems scapegoating: Minuchin, S. (structural family therapy; the "identified patient," scapegoating/detouring); Bowen, M. (triangulation; tension between two diverted onto a third). Established within the tradition; some empirical support for triangulation–adjustment links. Clinical/supported.
2. "Narcissistic family" literature: Donaldson-Pressman, S., & Pressman, R. (1994), *The Narcissistic Family*; McBride, K. (2008), *Will I Ever Be Good Enough?* (daughters of narcissistic mothers — gendered, don't over-generalize); Payson, E. (2002), *The Wizard of Oz and Other Narcissists*. Clinical/popular; vivid description, sparse controlled evidence. (Splitting / idealization–devaluation, Kernberg, is a theoretical bridge, not a demonstrated mechanism.)
3. Parental differential treatment (the load-bearing caveat): Suitor, J. J., & Pillemer, K. — Within-Family Differences Study (maternal favoritism common; harm tracks perceived unfairness/disfavoritism; last-borns disproportionately favored); Conger, K. — PDT and sibling adjustment. Supported.
4. The four-role taxonomy (hero/scapegoat/lost child/mascot): Wegscheider-Cruse, S. (1981), *Another Chance* — from addiction-family systems; clinically derived and **factor-analytically unsupported**. Contested/folk. Concept-creep on "narcissist": Haslam (2016); see [[epistemic-guardrails]].
