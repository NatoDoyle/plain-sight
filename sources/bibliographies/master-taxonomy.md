# Bibliography log — master-taxonomy

Sweep date: 2026-06-10. Mode: hybrid; prior-taxonomies verification agent returned full web-verified results.

## Verified against primary/secondary sources (web sweep)

- Buss et al. (1987) JPSP 52(6):1219–1229 — six tactics: charm, silent treatment, coercion, reason, regression, **debasement** (corrected from "self-abasement"). Buss (1992) *J. Personality* 60(2):477–499 — 12 tactics total (adds responsibility invocation, **reciprocity**, monetary reward, pleasure induction, social comparison, hardball).
- McCornack (1992) *Comm. Monographs* 59(1):1–16 — four Gricean maxims (quantity, quality, relation/relevance, manner); IMT2 = McCornack et al. (2014) *JLSP* 33(4):348–377.
- Buller & Burgoon (1996) *Communication Theory* 6(3):203–242 — falsification, concealment, equivocation. Critique: DePaulo, Ansfield & Bell (1996) same journal ("We cannot find the 'why' question").
- IPA (1937) "How to Detect Propaganda," *Propaganda Analysis* 1(2) — seven devices confirmed verbatim.
- Lifton (1961) ch. 22 — all eight criteria names confirmed.
- Hassan BITE — four components confirmed (freedomofmind.com); acronym formalized in *Releasing the Bonds* (2000); featured in 2015/2018 CCMC editions. Critique noted: Introvigne/Bitter Winter (partisan source flag) + APA DIMPAC memo (1987).
- Duluth wheel — 8 spokes confirmed (each "Using …"); physical/sexual violence is the **rim**, not a spoke. Critique: Dutton & Corvo (2007) *Aggression and Violent Behavior*.
- Cialdini 7 principles incl. unity (Pre-Suasion 2016; Influence New & Expanded 2021) — confirmed.
- Mathur et al. (2019) *PACM HCI* 3(CSCW) art. 81 — 15 types in 7 categories (sneaking, urgency, misdirection, social proof, scarcity, obstruction, forced action). Gray et al. (2018) CHI — 5 strategies (nagging, obstruction, sneaking, interface interference, forced action). Brignull deceptive.design — currently **16** types (originally ~12 in 2010). Gray, Santos, Bielova & Mildner (2024) CHI ontology consolidates; field shifting to "deceptive patterns" terminology.
- Krombholz et al. (2015) *JISA* 22:113–122 — channel/operator/type taxonomy. Mitnick 4-phase cycle (research; rapport & trust; exploit trust; utilize info) via Mouton et al. (2014).
- Marwell & Schmitt (1967) *Sociometry* 30(4):350–364 — 16 techniques (names confirmed). Kellermann & Cole (1994) *Communication Theory* 4(1):3–60 — 64 strategies consolidated from 74 schemes; their critique ("taxonomic disorder") noted.
- Simon *In Sheep's Clothing* — ~18 tactics (expanded from my 14: adds lying, evasion, feigning innocence, feigning confusion, brandishing anger). Count varies by edition (medium confidence on exact count).
- Whaley (1982) *J. Strategic Studies* 5(1):178–192 — dissimulation (masking, repackaging, dazzling) + simulation (mimicking, inventing, decoying).
- Stajano & Wilson (2011) *CACM* 54(3):70–75 — seven principles confirmed.
- Pratkanis (2007) in *The Science of Social Influence* — 107 tactics (+18 credibility/attraction).
- Kipnis, Schmidt & Wilkinson (1980) *J. Applied Psychology* 65(4):440–452 — eight dimensions confirmed.
- Fischer, Lea & Evans (2009) OFT1070 *The Psychology of Scams* — elements confirmed.
