# Bibliography log — memory-self-editing

Sweep date 2026-08-17 (4 parallel lenses: academic/empirical, skeptic/replication, exploitation/regulatory, recognition/phenomenology). Synthesis 2026-08-17. **Tooling note:** the session's WebSearch budget was exhausted before all four sweeps began, so every verification ran through direct API fetch — Crossref REST, OpenAlex (rate-limited to 429 partway through, Retry-After ≈ 10 h), Europe PMC REST, PubMed E-utilities, plus WebFetch of Wikipedia and BJS. ScienceDirect, Springer, PsycNet, Semantic Scholar, FTC and ResearchGate returned 403/429. Consequence: locators below are Crossref- or EuropePMC-verified, most quoted strings are verbatim from fetched abstracts, and everything unverified is flagged rather than smoothed over.

**Steer corrections adopted into the draft (sweeps override the steer):** (1) hindsight bias is *modest* — Md = .39 [.36, .42] — and two of the meta's moderators cut against the naive victim-blaming story (neutral outcomes gave *larger* effects than valenced ones; almanac items larger than real-world events), so the victim-blaming application rests on Carli 1999, not on the meta; (2) meta-analytic debiasing is a null and warnings specifically fail, in tension with Roese & Vohs's consider-the-alternative claim — both printed; (3) outcome bias got *bigger* under preregistration (N = 20 → N = 692) and persists in people who endorse the correct principle; (4) FAB is **reduced, not reversed**, in dysphoria/depression/higher PTSD symptoms — the steer's "reverses" was dropped, and the one documented reversal is the *narcissism* finding; (5) FAB is adaptive and its absence is the clinical marker, so the adaptive function is stated first and the file explicitly does not tell severely harmed targets "your brain deletes the pain"; (6) peak-end and duration neglect **dissociate** — duration neglect graded higher and the "abuse cycle runs on peak-end" inference explicitly refused as over-extension past the tested boundary; (7) self-consistency bias is *two-directional* (Ross's implicit theories: stability → "I always felt this way"; change → Conway & Ross's retroactively darkened "before"), which is the sharper and more manipulation-relevant claim; (8) the Allport-Postman problem is citation integrity, not "limitations" — the razor set-piece and the ~70% transmission-loss figure are not printed and the study is not used as eyewitness evidence; (9) the positivity-effect → elder-fraud inference is fenced with Ross, Grossmann & Schryer 2014 (new to the KB) and the `hypothesized` grade in `cognitive-decline-age` is inherited, not upgraded; (10) rosy retrospection has a **days-long** latency and is *polarized* (extremes amplified in both directions), not uniformly warm; (11) cryptomnesia and telescoping are `not-a-lever` — carried as false-positive guards, and said plainly. File grade `established` per the sibling convention (`pattern-illusions`, `self-evaluation-illusions`): the grade reflects the family core (hindsight, outcome bias, FAB, duration neglect, source monitoring), with every weaker member graded inline; the sweeps' alternative recommendation of `supported` as the aggregate is logged here as a defensible dissent. `safety: dv-escalation` adopted per three sweeps' recommendation.
