## Sources cited in dynamics/mlm-lifecycle.md

1. Taylor, J. M. (~2011). *The Case (For and) Against Multi-Level Marketing* (Consumer Awareness Institute; submitted as a public comment to the FTC Business Opportunity Rule rulemaking) — analysis of pay plans of 350+ MLMs; <1% of participants profit, ~99% lose money (≈99.6% averaged). Independent/advocacy-adjacent research, FTC-submitted (not an FTC finding).
2. FTC: 2011 Business Opportunity Rule (76 FR 76816) tailored to *exclude* MLMs; FTC enforcement statements ("substantial majority… lose money"); FTC staff report (2024) analyzing 70 MLM income-disclosure statements (majority of distributors make little or no money). Regulatory.
3. Cultic-dynamics lens: Hassan, S., BITE model applied to MLMs ("commercial cults"); anti-MLM literature — FitzPatrick, R. (*False Profits*; Pyramid Scheme Alert), Taylor (above), *The Dream* podcast (S1, 2018), r/antiMLM. Clinical/advocacy lens, not consensus.
4. Lifecycle/"tool scam" documentation: accounts of the Amway tools business and downline tool-profit structure; "inventory loading" / "garage qualifying" as established terms in case law and consumer-protection literature.
5. MLM vs pyramid legal line: *In re Koscot Interplanetary* (FTC, 1975); *Webster v. Omnitrition International* (9th Cir. 1996) — primacy of retail sales to ultimate (outside) users; enforced under Section 5 of the FTC Act. Application to specific MLMs is contested (cf. TINA.org). (US; not legal advice.)
