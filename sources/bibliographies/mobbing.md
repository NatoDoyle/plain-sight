## Sources cited in tactics/mobbing.md

1. Leymann, H. (1990). "Mobbing and Psychological Terror at Workplaces." *Violence and Victims* 5(2), 119–126 — the definition (systematic hostility, ≥weekly, ≥6 months, target rendered defenseless), the **LIPT** (~45 acts / 5 categories), the five-phase model (critical incident → stigmatization → management involvement → branding/"incorrect diagnosis" → expulsion), and the PTSD-comparable harm + the (dated, Swedish-data) suicide estimate. Foundational.
2. Einarsen, S., et al. — workplace bullying defined by repetition + power imbalance; the Negative Acts Questionnaire (NAQ-R). Nielsen, M. B., Matthiesen, S. B., & Einarsen, S. (2010), meta-analysis (~130,000 respondents): bullying exposure ~11.3% (self-label with definition), ~14.8% (behavioral measure), ~18.1% (self-label without definition) — prevalence is method-dependent. Supported.
3. Legal (jurisdiction-labeled; not legal advice): US has no general anti-mobbing/anti-bullying statute (lawful unless tied to a protected class — see [[workplace-bosses]]); Sweden's Ordinance on Victimisation at Work (1993) was the first; France's *harcèlement moral* provisions (2002) are among the strongest. Jurisdiction-/date-specific.
