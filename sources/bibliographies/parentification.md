## Sources cited in tactics/parentification.md

1. Boszormenyi-Nagy, I., & Spark, G. (1973). *Invisible Loyalties: Reciprocity in Intergenerational Family Therapy* — introduced/centered "parentification" within contextual family therapy (loyalty; the unfair "ledger"). Minuchin, S. (1974). *Families and Family Therapy* — the "parental child" / dissolved generational boundaries. Foundational family-systems theory.
2. Instrumental vs. emotional/affective parentification (emotional generally more harmful); long-term associations and resilience/PTG findings — Hooper, L. M. (Parentification Inventory; 2007 and later); reviews (e.g., *Frontiers in Psychology*, 2021). Supported (much correlational/clinical).
3. Jurkovic, G. (1997). *Lost Childhoods: The Plight of the Parentified Child* — "destructive parentification" as a discriminable form of maltreatment; markers = developmentally inappropriate, chronic, unacknowledged, unfair/non-reciprocal. The adaptive-vs-destructive hinge (acknowledgment + fairness). Clinical/supported.
4. Cultural variation: language/culture brokering as a normative care practice (e.g., Crafter, 2022, *Children & Society*); critique of Western-individualist over-pathologizing. With the caveat that brokering can still correlate with parentification overload. Supported.
