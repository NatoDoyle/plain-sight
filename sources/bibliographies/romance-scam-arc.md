## Sources cited in dynamics/romance-scam-arc.md

1. Whitty, M. T. (2013). "The Scammers Persuasive Techniques Model: Development of a Stage Model to Explain the Online Dating Romance Scam." *British Journal of Criminology* 53(4), 665–684 — the seven stages (motivation → ideal profile → grooming → sting → continuation → sexual abuse → re-victimization); foot-in-the-door first ask; grooming likened to other grooming. Peer-reviewed; qualitative (n≈20).
2. Whitty, M. T., & Buchanan, T. (2016). "The online dating romance scam: The psychological impact on victims — both financial and non-financial." *Criminology & Criminal Justice* — the "double hit"; relationship loss often experienced as worse than the financial loss (bereavement-like); victimization exploits normal trust/idealization, not stupidity.
3. Pig-butchering (sha zhu pan, 杀猪盘, "fatten before slaughter"): ~2016 China origin; romance + investment-fraud blend; run from trafficking-fueled compounds in Myanmar/Cambodia/Laos (many "scammers" are themselves trafficking victims). Convergent investigative/regulatory reporting (FBI, DOJ, Chainalysis/TRM, U.S. Secret Service).
4. Loss data (cite by year/agency; do not merge): FTC — ~$1.14B romance-scam losses, 2023 (median ~$2,000; ~64,000 reports). FBI IC3 2023 — ~$652M "confidence/romance"; investment fraud ~$4.6B (separate category). IC3 2024 — total losses >$16B; crypto-investment fraud ~$5.8B; elder (60+) losses elevated. Regulatory data.
