## Sources cited in tactics/stalkerware-monitoring.md

1. Citizen Lab, *The Predator in Your Pocket: A Multidisciplinary Assessment of the Stalkerware Application Industry* (2019); Coalition Against Stalkerware (founded Nov 2019; members incl. EFF, Kaspersky, NNEDV, Malwarebytes, NortonLifeLock, Operation Safe Escape). Apple/Google "unwanted tracker" detection (AirTag-stalking response; cross-platform alerts, 2024). Supported.
2. NNEDV Safety Net / techsafety.org and FTC Consumer Advice ("Stalkerware: What To Know"): check for/remove monitoring only after safety planning, from a device the abuser isn't monitoring; removal can escalate danger. The load-bearing safety guidance. Practitioner/regulatory.
3. Legal (US, jurisdiction-labeled; not legal advice): covert spyware/interception can violate the federal Wiretap Act (ECPA), the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act, and state stalking/eavesdropping laws (incl. all-party-consent states); enforcement is spotty and the apps occupy a legal gray market. FTC v. Support King/SpyFone (2021) — first FTC ban of a stalkerware operator from the surveillance business. Parental-of-minors and employer-of-company-device monitoring are context-dependent carve-outs.
