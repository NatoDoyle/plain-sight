"Increasing Compliance by Improving the Deal: The That's-Not-All Technique." *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology* 51(2), 277–283 (seven experiments; reciprocity + anchoring/contrast; deliberation reduces the effect). Replication debate: Frederick, Lee & Baskin (2014) and Yang & Lynn (2014) failed to reproduce with naturalistic stimuli; Huber et al. (2014) rebuttal; later real-world evidence.
