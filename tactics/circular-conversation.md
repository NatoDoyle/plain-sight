# Circular Conversation

> Arguments engineered never to resolve: topics rotate, definitions shift, settled points unsettle, and an hour later you're back at the beginning — exhausted, foggier, and somehow now the one apologizing. The conversation wasn't failing to reach a conclusion. Avoiding one was the point.

## Definition

Circular conversation is **conversational structure deployed to exhaust rather than resolve** — looping arguments, topic rotation, contradiction stacking, re-litigation of the settled, and definitional drift, sustained until the target concedes, apologizes, or gives up the original issue just to end the ordeal. The survivor lexicon calls it "word salad" (a term borrowed from psychiatry — see Caveats); practitioner DV literature describes the pattern plainly: circular conversations whose repetition "eventually wears the victim out and they give up in exhaustion" [3]. The construct is graded `clinical` — its documentation is practitioner and survivor-side — but its engine is verified mechanism: confusion measurably increases compliance with whatever resolution is attached ([[cognitive-overload-confusion]]'s disrupt-then-reframe), and depleted, flooded targets seize *any* closure ([[certainty-needs]]' urgency tendency, [[emotional-flooding]]). At its most sustained, inside controlling relationships, the cumulative effect feeds what Stark called **perspecticide** — "the abuse-related incapacity to 'know what you know'" (Stark's application of Suárez-Orozco's term) [1].

## Variants & aliases

- **The loop** — the same accusation re-raised freshly after each resolution, as if the resolving never happened ([[history-rewriting]] at conversation speed).
- **Topic rotation** — every approach toward the point spawns a new grievance to chase; the original issue dies of displacement ([[projection-blame-shifting]]'s counter-accusation engine supplying fresh topics).
- **Contradiction stacking** — positions swapped mid-argument ("I never said that / obviously I said that, you weren't listening"), keeping the target permanently re-orienting.
- **Definitional drift** — words re-meaning themselves mid-use; agreement becomes impossible because the terms won't hold still ([[moving-goalposts]] applied to language).
- **The filibuster** — sheer volume and talk-time domination; interruption and topic control as conversational power (the classic interruption-dominance research anchors the power reading, with replication caveats) [4].
- **Public-debate form** — the Gish gallop ([[bad-faith-argumentation]] owns the argumentative variants; this file owns the relational deployment).

## How it works

The target arrives with a point and an expectation: conversations resolve. The tactic weaponizes that expectation — each loop costs the target working memory, emotional regulation, and time ([[cognitive-overload-confusion]]; [[emotional-flooding]] as voices rise), while costing the operator little (they're not tracking coherence; incoherence *is* the instrument). Exhaustion converts to extraction at the exit: the depleted target accepts whatever framing ends it — an apology they don't owe, a dropped grievance, a "let's just forget it" that buries the original issue ([[certainty-needs]]: closure-seizing peaks precisely when depleted). Run chronically inside a closed relationship, the fog generalizes: the target stops trusting their ability to follow *any* disagreement — perspecticide's incapacity to know what you know [1] ([[gaslighting]]'s exhaustion-side companion).

## Recognition

### Behavioral markers

- **Resolution allergy**: settled points reliably un-settle; agreements reached at midnight are gone by morning; apologies obtained never close anything.
- **Your point never gets ten consecutive seconds**: every approach to it triggers a topic spawn, a counter-grievance, or a definitional dispute.
- **Position-swapping without acknowledgment** — they argue both sides across one conversation, with no embarrassment, because consistency isn't the game.
- **The conversation has a direction**: somehow it always travels *away* from their conduct and *toward* your flaws, your tone, your past.
- Time disproportion: three-hour arguments about sentences; conflicts that consume evenings and resolve nothing — recurring.

### Typical phrases

"That's not what we were talking about." · "I never said that." / "I obviously said that." · "Why are you bringing up the past?" (about the present issue) · "You always twist my words" (mid-twist of yours) · "We've been over this" (about the thing never resolved) · "I don't even know what you're upset about anymore — see, you don't either."

### Felt-sense indicators

- **Coming out knowing less than you went in** — the issue clear at minute one, unfindable by minute sixty.
- Rehearsing the conversation in advance and conducting a post-mortem after — *every time* — with this one person.
- The 2am surrender: apologizing or dropping it not because you agree but to be allowed to sleep ([[cognitive-overload-confusion]]'s concede-to-end-it signature).
- Fog localized to one relationship; articulate everywhere else ([[gaslighting]]'s localization tell — the two travel together).

### Escalation signs

Loops deployed at every accountability attempt (the conversational arm of [[darvo]]); sleep deprivation entering the pattern (arguments engineered to consume nights — a recognized coercive-control stressor); the fog generalizing into self-distrust (perspecticide territory [1]); third parties told you're "impossible to talk to" ([[smear-campaigns]] using the wreckage as evidence).

## Where it appears

Couples and families (the canonical late-night loop); workplaces (meetings where decisions dissolve, the colleague whose threads consume hours and settle nothing); high-control groups (doctrinal circularity — every question answered with the question's sinfulness — [[cults-high-control]], [[thought-terminating-cliches]] as the loop's exits); and public argument (the gallop — [[bad-faith-argumentation]]).

## Typical sequences & co-occurrence

Pairs with [[moving-goalposts]] (standards and definitions shifting mid-game) and [[projection-blame-shifting]] (the counter-accusation as topic-spawner); serves [[gaslighting]] (exhausted targets update easier — [[memory-fallibility]] under depletion) and [[darvo]] (the confrontation that gets lost in the maze never lands). The exhaustion it produces is the entry state for [[certainty-needs]] exploitation — and for the target's eventual [[jade-avoidance]] failure (over-explaining into the loop).

## Counter-strategies

### In the moment

- **One sentence, held** ([[jade-avoidance]]): pick the load-bearing point and repeat it, verbatim, without chasing spawned topics — "I hear you. The issue I raised is X." The loop requires your pursuit; a stationary target starves it.
- **Name the structure, not the content, once**: "We've circled three times. I'm stopping here; we can continue in writing." Content-engagement feeds it; structure-naming exits it.
- **Exit on depletion as policy** ([[emotional-flooding]]'s rule): no continuing past your capacity — "I'm done for tonight; my position stands" is a complete resolution. The 2am concession is the tactic's payday; refuse to fund it.

### Structural / long-term

- **Move conflicts to writing** ([[documentation-practices]]): asynchronous, serialized, on the record — the loop's three fuels (speed, volume, deniability) all die in text. Practitioner protocols converge here: Eddy's **BIFF** method — Brief, Informative, Friendly, Firm — is the field-tested template for responding to high-conflict communication without feeding it [2].
- **Track resolution rates** ([[manipulation-audit]]): of the last ten conflicts with this person, how many *stayed* resolved? A standing near-zero is structure, not chemistry.
- **Pre-commit conversational budgets**: "twenty minutes; if we're looping, we pause and write" — agreed in calm, enforced in storm ([[boundary-scripts]]).

### When to exit or escalate

Where the loops are the *only* mode — where no grievance of yours has ever survived a conversation intact — accountability is structurally impossible, which is the repair-channel fact that matters ([[darvo]]'s exit logic). Chronic versions inside controlling relationships (with sleep deprivation, generalized self-doubt) sit inside [[coercive-control]]: see `README.md` resources and [[dv-safety-planning]] rather than better debate tactics.

## Caveats & false positives

### Not circular conversation when…

- **Hard conversations naturally loop**: grief, big decisions, and genuine misunderstandings circle for honest reasons; two upset people re-explaining themselves is Tuesday, not tactics (*honest disagreement*). The diagnostic is *resolution allergy as a pattern* — loops that never close with one person, against a baseline of conversations that close with everyone else.
- **Disorganized ≠ strategic**: anxiety, ADHD, trauma responses, and plain poor communication produce rambling, tangents, and contradiction without design (*neurodivergent communication*, *incompetence not malice*). Function and benefit are the discriminators: who profits from the fog, and what happens when you ask to slow down ([[manipulation-vs-influence]]'s response-to-resistance)?
- **"Word salad" must never be aimed at disordered speech**: the term belongs to psychiatry (schizophrenia's formal thought disorder); applying this file's vocabulary to mental illness or disability is both wrong and cruel — the tactic-grade requires the fog *clearing instantly when the topic turns safe*, which disorder doesn't do.
- **You may be the loop**: pursued partners re-raising the same grievance nightly experience *themselves* as seeking resolution — run the symmetric check (would a neutral observer say I'm re-litigating settled things?) before filing charges ([[epistemic-guardrails]]).

### Base rates & severity calibration

Most maddening conversations are two flooded people with poor tools. The tactic-grade conjunction: **resolution allergy + direction (always away from their conduct) + safe-topic lucidity + extraction at the exit**, recurring ([[epistemic-guardrails]]; one awful argument is rung-1 evidence). And the label reverses, as always: "you're talking in circles" deployed to dismiss someone's legitimate persistence is its own silencing move ([[therapy-speak-weaponization]]).

## Evidence & debates

Graded `clinical` honestly: the named pattern lives in practitioner DV materials [3] and survivor phenomenology, not peer-reviewed taxonomy (the strongest qualitative coercive-control work documents cumulative wearing-down without naming circular argument as a theme). The *machinery* is verified — disruption-compliance, load, depletion-closure ([[cognitive-overload-confusion]], [[certainty-needs]]) — and the framing concepts have real anchors: perspecticide (Stark, via Suárez-Orozco) [1], conversational dominance (classic, with replication caveats) [4], and a field-tested counter-protocol (BIFF, practitioner grade) [2].
