# Isolation Tactics

> Every serious manipulation framework — the Duluth wheel, Stark's coercive control, Lifton's milieu control, Biderman's coercion chart — puts isolation at or near the top, because it is the *enabling* tactic: sever the target's other bonds and information channels, and every other lever pulls harder against no resistance.

## Definition

Isolation tactics are **the deliberate, progressive severing of a target's connections — people, information, money, mobility — until the manipulator mediates the target's world**. It is a named spoke of the Duluth Power & Control Wheel ("controlling what she does, who she sees and talks to, what she reads, where she goes, limiting her outside involvement, using jealousy to justify actions" [1]) and one of Stark's four core coercive-control tactic classes (violence, intimidation, **isolation**, control) [2]. It is *measured*: validated coercive-control instruments carry isolation as a distinct subscale [3]. And it generalizes: trafficked persons report isolation first among their controllers' coercion tactics [5]; high-control groups formalize it as milieu control ([[information-asymmetry]]); elder abuse runs on it in both directions — isolation enables abuse, and abusers isolate their victims [4].

## Variants & aliases

- **Social severing** — friends and family derogated ("they don't really care about you"), conflicts with them manufactured, visits sabotaged, until contact quietly lapses [2].
- **Jealousy-as-love framing** — surveillance and restriction sold as devotion ("I just love you too much to share you" — the Duluth wheel names this justification explicitly [1]).
- **Geographic isolation** — the move away from your people, often early and romantically framed ([[love-bombing]]'s relocation chapter).
- **Economic isolation** — transport, phone, and money controlled so leaving-the-house has logistics ([[financial-abuse]]; economic abuse instruments overlap [3]).
- **Digital isolation** — phone checked, passwords demanded, stalkerware installed, communications blocked or monitored ([[stalkerware-monitoring]]; documented capability set [6]).
- **Informational isolation** — milieu control: outside media, critics, and ex-members forbidden ([[cults-high-control]], [[information-asymmetry]]).
- **Preemptive social poisoning** — your reputation damaged with your own people so the bridge burns from both ends ([[smear-campaigns]]: "she's been unstable lately, don't take it personally").

## How it works

Isolation is the **force multiplier**. With outside bonds gone, [[attachment-needs]] concentrate on the one remaining supplier — the manipulator — making every other tactic ([[conditional-love]], [[silent-treatment]], [[gaslighting]]) bite harder. With outside information gone, reality-calibration dies — no one left to say "that's not normal": [[gaslighting]] requires that absence of outside calibration, and [[memory-fallibility]] supplies the malleable record it works on. The jealousy-as-love framing trades on [[trust-mechanics]] — restriction sold as benevolence ("I'm just protecting you") borrows the warmth cue to disguise the control. With money and mobility gone, exit becomes logistics ([[escalation-entrapment]]). The progression is typically **gradual and romantically deniable** — early togetherness reads as love; the trajectory documented across the coercive-control literature runs from preference ("stay with me instead") through friction (conflicts whenever you see them) to architecture (no car, no money, no one left to call) [2]. Trafficking research shows the same structure at maximum intensity, mapped onto Biderman's classic coercion framework with isolation as the lead tactic [5].

## Recognition

### Behavioral markers

- **Your circle shrinking on a schedule that tracks the relationship** — friendships that "naturally" lapsed all within a year of one person's arrival.
- **Cost attached to every outside contact**: sulks, interrogations, manufactured crises timed to your plans, days of [[silent-treatment]] after visits.
- Systematic **derogation of your people** — each friend and relative individually discredited ("she's jealous of us", "your brother disrespects me").
- **Logistics narrowing**: car access, phone, money, passwords progressively mediated by one person [3].
- They've engineered being your *only channel* to news, money, or the outside ([[information-asymmetry]]'s dependence-farming).

### Typical phrases

"Your friends don't really know you like I do." · "Your family has never treated you right — I'm just protecting you." · "Why do you need to see them? Aren't I enough?" · "I just love you too much to share you." · "She's a bad influence." · "Go ahead and go — but don't expect me to be okay." · (Group form:) "Outside people will only confuse your progress."

### Felt-sense indicators

- The dawning inventory: *who would I even call?* — and the answer has shrunk to one name.
- Pre-emptive social self-censorship: declining invitations to avoid the cost at home; editing what you tell friends so it can't be used later.
- Relief at the idea of seeing old friends, followed immediately by dread of the aftermath.
- The outside world feeling increasingly abstract, reported to you secondhand by one narrator.

### Escalation signs

From preference to surveillance (location demands, phone checks, [[stalkerware-monitoring]]); from friction to prohibition; money and transport formally controlled ([[financial-abuse]]); your remaining contacts fed the instability narrative ([[smear-campaigns]]) — the completed structure *is* [[coercive-control]], and physical risk rises with completeness (the abused elder whose only outside contact is a doctor [4]; the partner with no one left to notice).

## Where it appears

Intimate partner abuse — the canonical, measured arena [1][2][3]; families (the controlling parent isolating a child or adult child from peers and other relatives); high-control groups (milieu control as doctrine [[cults-high-control]]); trafficking [5]; elder abuse (shared living + low outside contact is the high-risk configuration; isolation is both tactic and risk factor [4]); and scams — romance-fraud scripts explicitly discourage telling family ("they won't understand us" — [[romance-scam-arc]]).

## Typical sequences & co-occurrence

Isolation **enables** the rest: it typically follows [[love-bombing]] (the intensity that made togetherness feel like devotion), travels with [[stalkerware-monitoring]], [[financial-abuse]], and [[smear-campaigns]], and is the precondition for [[gaslighting]] at full strength and [[coercive-control]] as a completed system. The jealousy-justification connects it to [[veiled-threats]] ("if I ever saw you with him…"). In groups, it pairs with [[unity-ingroup]]'s us-them hardening and the apostasy machinery around leavers.

## Counter-strategies

### In the moment

- **Treat derogation-of-your-people as data about the speaker** ([[detection-heuristics]]): one person systematically discrediting *everyone* you're attached to is a pattern with a known function — log it rather than litigating each friend's merits.
- **Don't pre-pay the cost**: declining your own plans to avoid their reaction is the mechanism completing itself; go, and let their response be visible information ([[manipulation-vs-influence]]'s response-to-no).

### Structural / long-term

- **Maintain bonds as policy, especially when it's expensive** — regular, *unannounced-as-negotiable* contact with friends and family; independent phone, accounts, and transport where possible ([[attachment-needs]]' diversification is the structural defense; [[financial-abuse]]'s counters overlap).
- **Keep one unmediated channel** — a person, an account, a device the partner/group doesn't control; in high-risk situations this is safety infrastructure, not disloyalty (see Safety notes).
- **Tell your people the truth early** ([[documentation-practices]]): isolation works partly by making the story unshareable later; friends who know the real picture in real time are calibration *and* lifeline.
- **Audit the trajectory annually**: list your active bonds now versus two years ago; shrinkage that tracks one relationship is the diagnostic, whatever each individual lapse felt like.

### When to exit or escalate

Completed or near-complete isolation is not a couples-communication issue — it is the architecture of [[coercive-control]], and it is the condition under which leaving is most dangerous (no witnesses, no resources, no logistics). Exit planning belongs with professionals who do this daily: see Safety notes. Rebuilding the severed network is also the recovery path — bonds resume more readily than isolation made you believe ([[recovery-rebuilding]]).

## Caveats & false positives

### Not isolation tactics when…

- **New-relationship absorption** — couples naturally cocoon early; the innocent version is *symmetric, temporary, and cost-free* (no punishment when you surface for your friends).
- **Legitimate concerns about specific people** — "your friend drives drunk with you in the car" is a *specific, evidence-bearing* objection that survives discussion; the tactic version is *systematic and universal* (everyone you're close to, eventually).
- **Introversion and life-stage shrinkage** — networks thin with moves, kids, and age on their own; the diagnostic is the *cost structure and the narrator*, not the headcount.
- **Your own protective distance** ([[self-protective-distance]]) — stepping back from genuinely harmful family or friends, including at a partner's sincere urging, is boundary-setting; the difference is who benefits, whether the evidence is real, and whether *other* bonds are encouraged or also targeted.

### Base rates & severity calibration

Wanting more of a partner's time is universal; jealousy is common and usually managed, not weaponized. The tactic-grade conjunction: **systematic derogation across your network + cost attached to contact + channel monopolization + trajectory over time** ([[epistemic-guardrails]]). One sulk about a night out is rung-1 evidence. And the label reverses: controllers accuse *targets* of isolation — "you're isolating me from your life", "your boundaries are cutting our family off" — framing the target's healthy limits as the crime ([[therapy-speak-weaponization]]; the accusation carries the same evidence standard as the behavior). Severity, though, runs the other way at the top end: this file is rated `critical` because completed isolation is the strongest single structural predictor that everything else in this KB will work unopposed.

## Safety notes

Isolation is core [[coercive-control]] architecture and a marker of elevated danger — by design it removes the witnesses, resources, and logistics that exits require. If this file describes your situation: contact professionals from a device and account the other person cannot see (a friend's phone, a work computer, a library). **US:** National Domestic Violence Hotline 1-800-799-7233 / thehotline.org (chat available) · **UK:** 0808 2000 247 · full list in `README.md`; protocol at [[dv-safety-planning]]. For elders or dependent adults isolated by caregivers, Adult Protective Services (US) or local safeguarding teams take third-party reports — the isolated person's silence is expected, not consent [4].

## Evidence & debates

`established`: isolation is named, theorized, and *measured* across independent frameworks and instruments — Duluth [1], Stark [2], validated subscales [3], elder-abuse reviews [4], trafficking coercion research [5], and the stalkerware technical literature [6]. The open territory is process detail: the full romantic-to-architectural progression is assembled from the coercive-control literature as a whole rather than enumerated in any single study (noted honestly; the qualitative anchor is post-separation persistence of nonviolent control [2]).
