# Love Bombing

> Overwhelming affection, attention, and commitment delivered faster than acquaintance can justify — not as love's overflow but as an *accelerant*: attachment installed before judgment arrives, so that everything that follows is paid for in fear of losing it.

## Definition

Love bombing is the **front-loading of intense affection, validation, gifts, and future-promises early in a relationship** — romantic or group — at a pace and volume calibrated to create rapid attachment and obligation. The term is the recruiters' own: coined inside the Unification Church in the 1970s (Moon used "love bomb" in a 1978 speech) and documented critically by Margaret Singer, who defined it as "a coordinated effort… flooding recruits and newer members with flattery, verbal seduction, affectionate but usually nonsexual touching, and lots of attention to their every remark" [1]. In couples, the practitioner consensus markers are: declarations ("I love you", "soulmate") within days or weeks, lavish early gifts — especially with strings attached — overwhelming contact volume, and commitment pressure on a compressed timeline [4]. The diagnostic core is not intensity but **function**: the affection is the *setup*, and its later rationing is the *control* ([[conditional-love]], [[intermittent-reinforcement]]).

## Variants & aliases

- **Romantic love bombing** — the whirlwind courtship that wants to move in, merge finances, or marry on a schedule no acquaintance supports.
- **Group love bombing** — the recruitment dinner where everyone is thrilled you exist; instant family [1] ([[unity-ingroup]], [[cult-conversion-funnel]]).
- **Scam-stage love bombing** — the documented grooming phase of romance fraud: declarations, marriage talk, and round-the-clock intensity for weeks before the first money request [3] ([[romance-scam-arc]]).
- **Re-bombing** — the same flood deployed at exits and after escapes ([[hoovering]]'s warm variant).
- **Parental/familial bursts** — extravagant affection alternating with withdrawal as a compliance thermostat ([[conditional-love]]'s cycle-mate).

## How it works

The lever stack: [[attachment-needs]] (supply the need fast, make it feel scarce, then price it), [[liking-similarity]] and [[mirroring-false-identity]] (the bomber is uncannily *your type* — often because the persona was assembled from your disclosures), [[reciprocity]] (gifts and intensity create debts: "after everything I've given you"), and — once the flood ebbs — [[intermittent-reinforcement]]: the early high becomes the jackpot the target works to recover. Velocity itself is functional: commitments extracted during the hot state ([[emotional-flooding]]) precede the information that would have prevented them, and the installed attachment raises the cost of believing later red flags ([[dissonance-exploitation]]).

## Recognition

### Behavioral markers

- **Intensity outrunning acquaintance**: soulmate language, exclusivity pressure, and big commitments (keys, moves, money) inside weeks [4].
- **Gifts with strings**: lavishness later invoked as obligation ("I bought this, so…" — the loveisrespect marker verbatim [4]).
- **Contact saturation**: continuous texts/calls framed as devotion, with withdrawal noticed and punished.
- **The script feels pre-written**: you're cast in a role ("I've never met anyone like you") that doesn't depend much on who you actually are.
- **Pace-setting resistance**: your attempts to slow down met with hurt, pressure, or accusations of coldness — the response-to-no test ([[manipulation-vs-influence]]).

### Typical phrases

"I've never felt like this — and it's only been a week." · "We don't need to wait; when you know, you know." · "You're my soulmate / other half / the one." · "Why do you need space from someone who loves you this much?" · "After everything I've done for you already…" · (Group form:) "You're home now. We're your real family."

### Felt-sense indicators

- Flattered *and* faintly suffocated — the gut "too good to be true" flag the DV hotlines name explicitly [4].
- Feeling behind on a schedule you never set; guilt for wanting ordinary pacing.
- Your friends' raised eyebrows registering as threats to the romance rather than as data ([[isolation-tactics]] beginning in your own head).
- After the flood ebbs: working to win back a warmth you never had to earn at first ([[intermittent-reinforcement]]'s signature).

### Escalation signs

The flood funding demands: exclusivity → isolation from "people who don't get us" → entanglement (cohabitation, accounts, secrets) on their timeline ([[escalation-entrapment]]) → the warm/cold cycle as standing control ([[conditional-love]], [[abuse-cycle]]'s reconciliation phase). In groups: the dinner becomes the weekend retreat becomes the escalating commitment ladder ([[commitment-consistency]], [[cult-conversion-funnel]]).

## Where it appears

Dating and early relationships (especially app-mediated, where profile-mining feeds the mirror — [[mirroring-false-identity]]); cult and high-control recruitment (the founding context [1]); romance fraud's grooming stage, documented in the stage model of the scam [3]; trafficking recruitment (survivor samples report it as a grooming tactic — emerging research [2]); and families that run affection as a thermostat.

## Typical sequences & co-occurrence

The canonical arc: **love-bomb → entangle → isolate → ration**. It precedes [[isolation-tactics]] and [[conditional-love]] in most documented abuse trajectories, pairs with [[future-faking]] (the promised future is part of the flood) and [[boundary-testing]] (early "small" overrides dressed as passion), and sets up [[trauma-bonding]]'s required ingredients (intensity + later intermittency). Its echo after escape is [[hoovering]].

## Counter-strategies

### In the moment

- **Set the pace and watch the response** ([[boundary-scripts]]): "I like you, and I move slower than this." A sincere admirer adjusts with grace; a bomber escalates, sulks, or reframes your pacing as rejection — that response is the data ([[manipulation-vs-influence]]).
- Accept the feeling, defer the commitments: enjoy warmth without signing anything — relocation, exclusivity, money, secrets — on flood-time ([[emotional-flooding]]'s no-decisions-hot rule).

### Structural / long-term

- **Velocity policy** ([[verification-rituals]]): pre-set personal timelines for milestone decisions (meeting friends, keys, money mixing) *before* you're in the hot state; treat schedule-pressure against them as a flag, not flattery.
- **Keep your calibrators close**: the friends whose eyebrows rose are your reality anchors — bombers' next move is removing them ([[isolation-tactics]]); pre-commit to not litigating new relationships against old friendships.
- **Audit the mirror**: "uncannily perfect for me" warrants the question of what they could have known in advance ([[liking-similarity]]'s coincidence audit; reverse-image, verify, video-call for online connections [3]).
- **Group version**: instant-family warmth gets the apostasy test — how does this group treat leavers? ([[unity-ingroup]], [[cult-exit-support]]).

### When to exit or escalate

If pacing requests reliably produce punishment, if isolation pressure follows the warmth, or if the warm/cold cycle has begun — the flood was infrastructure, and the relationship trajectory is the documented one ([[coercive-control]] risk rises after entanglement). Exit gets harder with every shared lease, account, and secret: leave *earlier* than feels emotionally warranted, and see Safety notes if control or threats appear.

## Caveats & false positives

### Not love bombing when…

- **Mutual [[limerence]]** — naturally intense early infatuation, *symmetric and unscripted*, that respects pacing requests and survives them. Intensity alone is the worst single cue in this file.
- **Cultural and personal courtship styles** — fast timelines are conventional in some cultures and temperaments ([[cultural-difference]]); generous gift-giving is many people's sincere love language ([[genuine-generosity]]: no strings, survives non-repayment).
- **New-relationship enthusiasm** with ordinary insecurity — texting a lot because smitten, not as saturation strategy. The discriminators: **response to pacing requests, strings on the generosity, and what follows the flood** (steady warmth vs rationing).

### Base rates & severity calibration

Most whirlwind romances are just whirlwind romances; some marry happily. The construct's evidence base is thin — practitioner consensus and the cult-recruitment record more than peer-reviewed study [2] — so this file runs at `clinical` grade and its conclusions should too: pattern + strings + pace-punishment + the isolation sequel, never the intensity alone ([[epistemic-guardrails]]).

## Safety notes

Love bombing that has progressed to isolation, monitoring, or punishment cycles is on the documented path into [[coercive-control]]: the severity override applies ([[epistemic-guardrails]]). Exit planning with professionals — **US:** 1-800-799-7233 / thehotline.org (whose own materials cover love bombing [4]) · **UK:** 0808 2000 247 · list in `README.md`; protocol at [[dv-safety-planning]]. Expect [[hoovering]] floods after leaving; pre-decide your response.

## Evidence & debates

Grade `clinical`, stated plainly: the founding documentation is cult-recruitment testimony and Singer's clinical observation [1]; the romantic-context literature remains thin — one exploratory undergraduate-journal study (correlations with narcissism and insecure attachment) and scattered low-tier papers [2]; the strongest adjacent evidence is the romance-scam stage model, where love-bombing-like grooming is well documented [3], plus the DV organizations' practitioner consensus [4]. The "idealize → devalue → discard" cycle vocabulary is pop-clinical (Durvasula et al.) without peer-reviewed validation as a named cycle — this KB uses its observations, graded folk [5]. Mechanism support, by contrast, is strong: every lever this tactic pulls is independently established.
