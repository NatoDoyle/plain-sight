# Proxy Recruitment

> Deploying other people to do the manipulation for you — to pressure, monitor, message, or smear a target on your behalf. The recruits are often well-meaning, fed a false story, and convinced they're helping. The manipulator stays clean; the target faces a crowd.

## Definition

Proxy recruitment is **enlisting third parties — friends, family, colleagues, even institutions — to pressure, surveil, relay messages to, or attack a target on the manipulator's behalf**. The folk label, "flying monkeys," comes from the Wizard of Oz (the witch's dispatched servants) and circulates in narcissistic-abuse communities; it has **no peer-reviewed literature under that name** — graded `clinical` on its neighbors, not the label. The strongest neighbor is rigorous: bullying-as-a-group-process research identifies **participant roles** beyond bully-and-victim — *assistants* and *reinforcers* who amplify the aggressor — showing recruited supporters are a structural feature of how aggression scales [1]. Stalking research documents **proxy stalking**: a pursuer using others, often *unwitting*, to monitor or contact a target [2]. The defining feature, and the source of its cruelty: the proxies are frequently **deceived rather than malicious** — recruited by a sympathetic false account into believing they're the ones helping a victim.

## Variants & aliases

- **The messenger army** — relayed contact a target has cut off ("your mother says call her," "everyone thinks you're being unfair") — defeating no-contact via third parties ([[hoovering]] by proxy; [[triangulation]]).
- **The pressure chorus** — friends/family deployed to lobby the target back into compliance ("you're breaking your father's heart," "the team needs you") — manufactured [[social-proof]] of the manipulator's position.
- **The smear delivery network** — proxies spreading the manipulator's distorted narrative, making it sound like independent consensus ([[smear-campaigns]]; many mouths = manufactured agreement).
- **Proxy surveillance** — others recruited to watch, report on, or locate the target ([[stalkerware-monitoring]] adjacent; proxy stalking [2]).
- **Institutional proxies** — courts, HR, CPS, police, schools weaponized via false or distorted complaints ("legal-systems abuse" [3]) — using systems as the attacking third party.
- **The recruited child** — children conscripted as messengers, spies, or pressure ([[triangulation]], Duluth "using children").

## How it works

Two levers. **Trust-mechanics**: the manipulator's *own* credibility — the sympathetic, wronged-sounding account — is what recruits the proxy; the third party acts on a story they trust without hearing the other side ([[trust-mechanics]]; the recruit is a target of manipulation too). **Social proof**: multiple people carrying the same message manufactures the appearance of independent consensus — the target faces what looks like *everyone's* judgment, not one person's ([[social-proof]]; this is why proxies are the delivery system for [[smear-campaigns]]). The structural payoff is **deniability and amplification at once**: the manipulator's hands stay clean (they "never said that"; the proxies are "just concerned"), while the pressure multiplies and the target is isolated against a crowd ([[isolation-tactics]]). Side-taking research notes third parties pick sides by self-interest, moral, and relational motives [4] — a sympathetic false account hijacks all three.

## Recognition

### Behavioral markers

- **Pressure arriving via people, not the source**: the manipulator's messages, demands, and judgments reach you through a rotating cast of intermediaries.
- **The proxies all have the same (one-sided) story** — fed from a single source, often with details only the manipulator would frame that way ([[smear-campaigns]]).
- **"Concerned" framing**: recruits present as worried-on-your-behalf or neutral peacemakers while carrying the manipulator's agenda ("I'm just the messenger").
- **Recruitment of your own people**: mutual friends, your family, your colleagues turned into channels — defeating distance and isolating you ([[isolation-tactics]]).
- **The manipulator stays deniable**: they didn't contact you, didn't say that — the proxies did.

### Typical phrases

(From the recruited proxy:) "I'm not taking sides, but have you considered…" · "They're really hurting — won't you just talk to them?" · "Everyone thinks you're being too harsh." · "I shouldn't be telling you this, but they wanted you to know…" · "We're all worried about you." · (From the recruiter, to proxies:) "I'm so worried about them — can you check on them / talk to them for me?"

### Felt-sense indicators

- Facing a crowd that's somehow all reciting one person's grievance.
- The disorientation of being lobbied by people you trust on behalf of someone you've stepped back from.
- Suspecting that confiding in a mutual friend means confiding in the manipulator.
- Guilt manufactured at one remove — the proxies' "concern" doing the [[guilt-leverage]] the manipulator can't do directly.

### Escalation signs

Proxies multiplying or escalating from gentle lobbying to pressure and ostracism; recruitment widening to your workplace or family; migration to institutional proxies (false reports, vexatious complaints — [[institutions]], legal-systems abuse [3]); proxy *surveillance* (others reporting your location/activity — proxy stalking [2], a safety matter). Children being recruited is both an escalation and a documented harm ([[triangulation]]).

## Where it appears

Intimate and post-separation abuse (recruited friends, family, and children — [[family-parents]]); families (the scapegoat pressured by relatives carrying the manipulator's narrative — [[golden-child-scapegoat]]); workplaces (the bully's assistants and reinforcers [1] — [[mobbing]]); and high-control groups, where the entire membership can be mobilized against a doubter or defector ([[cults-high-control]], [[unity-ingroup]]'s apostasy machinery, shunning by proxy).

## Typical sequences & co-occurrence

Proxy recruitment is the **deployed-army escalation of [[triangulation]]** (declared there) and the **delivery system for [[smear-campaigns]]** (declared there) — the proxies spread the distorted narrative and apply the pressure. It serves [[hoovering]] (reaching a no-contact target through others) and [[isolation-tactics]] (turning the target's own network against them). It pairs with [[guilt-leverage]] (the proxies carry the guilt) and [[victim-posturing]] (the sympathetic false account that recruits them).

## Counter-strategies

### In the moment

- **Decline the message, kindly, at the messenger** ([[boundary-scripts]]): "I appreciate you care, but if they have something to say to me, they can say it directly — I won't discuss it through you." This closes the channel without fighting the (often well-meaning) proxy.
- **Don't relay back**: refuse to send messages *through* the proxy either — keep the triangle closed in both directions ([[triangulation]]'s counter).
- **Recognize the recruit may be a target too**: the proxy is often deceived, not malicious — separating the person from the function keeps you from burning salvageable relationships.

### Structural / long-term

- **Inform your network preemptively**: tell the people who matter you don't want messages relayed or to be lobbied on someone's behalf — and, where a [[smear-campaigns]] is likely, give them your calm first-hand account so a one-sided story can't recruit them ([[smear-campaigns]]' counter).
- **Get the other side to the proxies**: many recruits drop out the moment they hear the account they weren't given — the manufactured consensus depends on one-sidedness ([[social-proof]]'s audit).
- **Document proxy contact and surveillance** ([[documentation-practices]]): patterns of relayed contact and monitoring can matter legally (harassment, stalking-by-proxy) and clarify the orchestration for you.
- **For institutional proxies**: respond to false reports/complaints with records through official channels; legal-systems abuse is countered with documentation and counsel, not with engaging the proxy [3].

### When to exit or escalate

Proxy *surveillance* (others recruited to monitor or locate you) is stalking-adjacent and a safety matter — see Safety notes. Institutional weaponization (false reports, vexatious litigation) needs records and counsel. Where proxies are mobilizing your whole community against you, the relationship/group has become the threat; protect your direct ties independently and consider [[no-contact-exit-planning]] / [[cult-exit-support]].

## Caveats & false positives

### Not proxy recruitment when…

- **People legitimately took sides after hearing a true account**: friends who side against someone because they accurately learned what that person did are exercising judgment, not being weaponized ([[honest-disagreement]]). The discriminators: **were the proxies deceived/given a one-sided story, and is there orchestration** — versus people independently concluding from the truth.
- **A real warning relayed** ([[genuine-warning]]): someone passing along a sincere, true caution ("she mentioned she's worried about you") is care, not recruitment.
- **Your OWN support network**: friends and family who advocate *for you*, check on you, or help you are your support — not proxies. (Manipulators invert this, calling a victim's genuine supporters "flying monkeys" — see below.)
- **Ordinary mutual-friend awkwardness**: shared networks carry messages and gossip organically after any conflict; the tactic-grade is *orchestration + one-sided narrative + the recruiter's deniability*, not the existence of intermediaries.

### Base rates & severity calibration

Most after-conflict involvement of third parties is organic side-taking and mutual-friend awkwardness, not a recruited army. The tactic-grade conjunction: **a recruiter feeding proxies a one-sided/false account + coordinated pressure or relay + the recruiter staying deniable** ([[epistemic-guardrails]]; one mutual friend passing a message is rung-1 evidence). And the label inverts dangerously: "flying monkeys" is readily turned against a victim's *real* supporters by an abuser who recasts their network as a conspiracy ([[therapy-speak-weaponization]], [[darvo]]) — the accusation owes the same evidence (deception of the proxies, orchestration) it claims.

## Safety notes

Proxy *surveillance* — third parties recruited to monitor, locate, or report on a target — is a form of stalking and a genuine danger signal, especially post-separation [2]. If you're being watched or located through others, treat it as a safety matter: document it, and plan with professionals. **US:** National Domestic Violence Hotline 1-800-799-7233 / thehotline.org · **UK:** 0808 2000 247 · list in `README.md`; protocol at [[dv-safety-planning]]. Institutional proxies (false reports to police/CPS, vexatious litigation) are legal-systems abuse — seek counsel and keep records.

## Evidence & debates

Graded `clinical`: the "flying monkeys" label is folk, but the pattern rests on solid neighbors — the participant-roles research showing recruited assistants/reinforcers amplify aggression [1], documented proxy stalking with often-unwitting third parties [2], side-taking motives in conflict [4], and the legal-systems-abuse literature [3]. The operational distinction the caveats turn on — *deceived proxies + orchestration* vs people legitimately siding from the truth, vs a victim's own network — is largely the KB's own, grounded in the *unwitting* nature of recruited proxies [2] and side-taking research [4]. The construct's main risk is reversibility (weaponizing "flying monkeys" against genuine supporters), which the caveats foreground.
