# Felt-Sense Index

> **Start here if you don't know the name of what's happening — only how it feels.** This is a reverse lookup: find the sentence that sounds like your experience, follow the links to candidate patterns, **then read each one's *Caveats & false positives* section before concluding anything.** A feeling is a *prompt to investigate*, never a verdict (see [[epistemic-guardrails]]). Many of these feelings have ordinary, innocent causes — that's why every entry names what it *could also be*.

## How to use this index

1. Find the felt-sense line closest to yours (Ctrl/Cmd-F your own words: "confused," "eggshells," "guilty," "too fast").
2. Open the **candidate patterns** — read their **Recognition** sections to see if the markers match.
3. **Read the "could also be" link and the file's Caveats** — rule out the innocent explanation honestly.
4. If it fits, follow the pattern's **Counter-strategies** and the defenses linked at the bottom.
5. If you feel **afraid, trapped, or unsafe**, skip straight to [[dv-safety-planning]] and the Safety line below.

## The index

### "I feel confused or disoriented after talking — like I'm losing my grip on reality"
**Candidates:** [[gaslighting]] · [[circular-conversation|circular conversation / word salad]] · [[history-rewriting]] · [[double-binds]] · [[moving-goalposts]]
**Could also be:** *memory divergence* or *honest disagreement* — two people genuinely remember/see it differently, with no one rewriting reality. → [[documentation-practices]]

### "I'm always walking on eggshells / bracing for the next blow-up"
**Candidates:** [[coercive-control]] · [[intermittent-reinforcement]] · [[silent-treatment]] · [[veiled-threats]] · [[abuse-cycle]] · [[emotional-flooding]]
**Could also be:** *normal relationship conflict* or someone going through a genuinely hard stretch — tension that is mutual, discussable, and not enforced by fear. → [[detection-heuristics]]

### "I feel guilty all the time / responsible for their feelings"
**Candidates:** [[guilt-tripping]] · [[emotional-blackmail]] · [[self-harm-threats]] · [[conditional-love]] · [[pity-plays]]
**Targets the vulnerability:** [[people-pleasing-fawn]]
**Could also be:** *honest hurt* — a person naming real pain you caused, without using it to control you. → [[boundary-scripts]]

### "Somehow I'm always the bad guy — I came to complain and ended up apologizing"
**Candidates:** [[darvo]] · [[projection-blame-shifting]] · [[reactive-abuse-provocation]] · [[victim-posturing]] · [[non-apology-patterns]]
**Could also be:** *genuine victimhood* — sometimes the other person really was hurt, or you really were (partly) wrong. Check who has power and who keeps the harm. → [[detection-heuristics]]

### "It moved so fast / it feels too good to be true / I was swept off my feet"
**Candidates:** [[love-bombing]] · [[future-faking]] · [[mirroring-false-identity]] · [[romance-scam-arc]] · [[charm-offensive]]
**Could also be:** *limerence* or *genuine rapport* — real early-relationship intensity and real compatibility exist. The tell is pressure, idealization, and speed that *resists slowing down*. → [[verification-rituals]]

### "I feel pressured to decide RIGHT NOW"
**Candidates:** [[manufactured-urgency]] · [[scarcity-urgency]] · [[foot-in-the-door]] · [[dark-patterns-social-urgency]] · [[four-square-payment-packing]] · [[bad-faith-negotiation]]
**Could also be:** *genuine deadline* or *genuine opportunity* — some deadlines are real. The test: does it survive you saying "I need to sleep on it"? → [[detection-heuristics]]

### "I feel smaller, drained, never quite good enough around them"
**Candidates:** [[negging]] · [[conditional-love]] · [[flattery-ego]] (praise that comes and goes) · [[triangulation]] (constant comparison)
**Look at the actor:** [[narcissistic-patterns]]
**Could also be:** *honest feedback* or your own insecurity — real feedback is specific, consistent, and meant to help you, not to keep you off balance. → [[gray-rock]]

### "I feel cut off from my friends and family"
**Candidates:** [[isolation-tactics]] · [[coercive-control]] · [[cult-conversion-funnel]] · [[financial-abuse]] (no means to leave)
**Targets the vulnerability:** [[loneliness-isolation]]
**Could also be:** *self protective distance* (yours, by choice) or natural life drift. The tell is *someone else engineering* the isolation. → [[helping-others]] (for a worried friend) · [[no-contact-exit-planning]]

### "I feel watched, tracked, or checked up on"
**Candidates:** [[stalkerware-monitoring]] · [[coercive-control]]
**Could also be:** *consensual monitoring* or *shared financial management* — monitoring both freely agreed to and can revoke. The tell is secrecy, coercion, or no opt-out. → **SAFETY:** [[dv-safety-planning]] (assume devices may be monitored)

### "I want to believe it so much — the returns/opportunity are incredible"
**Candidates:** [[hope-greed]] · [[ponzi-psychology]] · [[affinity-fraud]] · [[mlm-recruitment-scripts]] · [[romance-scam-arc]] (pig-butchering) · [[future-faking]]
**Could also be:** *genuine opportunity* or *legitimate small business* — but verify independently; legitimate offers welcome scrutiny and "no." → [[verification-rituals]]

### "They're so charming and everyone loves them — but something's off"
**Candidates:** [[charm-offensive]] · [[mirroring-false-identity]] · [[triangulation]]
**Look at the actor:** [[psychopathic-patterns]] · [[con-artist-typologies]]
**Could also be:** *genuine charisma* — charm is not a crime. Watch the *gap* between charm in public and conduct in private. → [[universal-red-flags]]

### "My gut says something is wrong, but I can't explain why"
**Candidates / start:** [[universal-red-flags]] · [[detection-heuristics]] · [[trust-mechanics]] · [[betrayal-blindness]]
**Note:** Gut unease is *data* (de Becker, *The Gift of Fear*) — not proof, but worth investigating rather than overriding. → [[manipulation-audit]]

### "I feel afraid to leave — like something bad will happen if I do"
**Candidates:** [[coercive-control]] · [[trauma-bonding]] · [[veiled-threats]] · [[self-harm-threats]] · [[escalation-entrapment]]
**This is a safety matter, not just an analysis one.** Leaving is the highest-risk window. → [[dv-safety-planning]] · [[no-contact-exit-planning]] · Safety line below.

### "I can never win an argument / they bury me in points / nothing ever gets resolved"
**Candidates:** [[circular-conversation]] · [[moving-goalposts]] · [[bad-faith-argumentation]] (sealioning, gish gallop, motte-and-bailey) · [[thought-terminating-cliches]] · [[history-rewriting]]
**Could also be:** *honest disagreement* — a real, good-faith disagreement that's simply unresolved. → [[jade-avoidance]] (stop over-explaining)

### "Just when I try to pull away, they pull me back in"
**Candidates:** [[hoovering]] · [[intermittent-reinforcement]] · [[breadcrumbing]] · [[future-faking]] · [[non-apology-patterns]]
**Why it's so sticky:** [[trauma-bonding]]
**Could also be:** *genuine reconciliation* — real repair involves changed behavior over time, not just words at the moment you're leaving. → [[no-contact-exit-planning]]

### "I feel special and chosen — this group/person finally *gets* me"
**Candidates:** [[love-bombing]] · [[cult-conversion-funnel]] · [[unity-ingroup]] · [[radicalization-pipeline]] · [[we-are-family-rhetoric]]
**Could also be:** *genuine community* or *legitimate religion* — belonging is a real human good. The markers of harm are deception, exploitation, and *punished exit* — not intensity or sincerity. → [[cult-exit-support]]

### "I'm overwhelmed by the paperwork/terms/details and just want it to be over"
**Candidates:** [[cognitive-overload-confusion]] · [[bad-faith-negotiation]] (snow job) · [[dark-patterns-obstruction]] · [[thats-not-all-decoys]] · [[four-square-payment-packing]]
**Could also be:** genuinely complex terms. The tell is complexity *engineered* to exhaust you near the signature. → [[verification-rituals]] (slow down, get it in writing)

### "I'm hearing bad things about everyone / being turned against people"
**Candidates:** [[triangulation]] · [[smear-campaigns]] · [[proxy-recruitment]] · [[isolation-tactics]]
**Could also be:** real interpersonal information honestly shared. The tell is a hub who keeps everyone divided and routed through them. → [[documentation-practices]]

### "A fog of obligation, guilt, and fear runs the relationship" (FOG)
**Candidate:** [[emotional-blackmail]] (Forward's FOG) · [[veiled-threats]] · [[self-harm-threats]] · [[guilt-tripping]]
**Could also be:** *honest hurt* or a relationship under genuine strain — obligation, guilt, and fear can arise without anyone engineering them; the tell is whether they're *used* to steer your choices.
→ [[boundary-scripts]] · [[detection-heuristics]]

### "I feel responsible for adult problems that aren't mine / I was the parent growing up"
**Candidates:** [[parentification]] · [[golden-child-scapegoat]] · [[conditional-love]] · [[guilt-tripping]]
**Could also be:** *age appropriate responsibility* — chores and helping out are healthy; carrying a parent's emotional or practical role is not. → [[recovery-rebuilding]]

### "An adult or older peer gives my child (or me, as a teen) special attention, gifts, and secret one-on-one time"
**Candidates:** [[grooming-sequence]] · [[boundary-testing]] (progressive boundary erosion) · [[isolation-tactics]]
**This is a safety matter, not just an analysis one.** Trust the unease; involve other adults; refuse the "our little secret" frame.
**Could also be:** *ordinary mentorship* — real mentors welcome transparency and parental involvement, and keep contact open, not secret. The tells are secrecy, engineered alone-time, and escalating boundary-testing.
→ **SAFETY:** [[grooming-sequence]] · **Childhelp 1-800-422-4453**; if a child is in immediate danger, **911**.

### "I should have seen it coming — how did I miss it?"
**Candidates:** [[memory-self-editing]] (hindsight bias makes the past look more predictable than it was) · [[why-smart-people-fall]] · [[attribution-errors]] (the blame that gets aimed at you, and that you aim at yourself)
**Read this first:** foreseeability and fault are different questions. Even where a warning really was there, responsibility sits with the person who did the thing. "How did you not see it?" is a recognized failure mode, not an insight.
**Could also be:** *memory divergence* — you may simply be remembering an ambiguous moment as a clear one now that you know how it ended; and undated, summary-shaped recall is normal, not evidence of anything. → [[documentation-practices]] · [[recovery-rebuilding]]

### "The good times feel amazing now that I'm out / I keep remembering the best of them"
**Candidates:** [[memory-self-editing]] (rosy retrospection and fading affect — the sting fades faster than the warmth) · [[hoovering]] · [[abuse-cycle]] · [[intermittent-reinforcement]]
**Read this first:** a memory of fear that has lost its charge is a predictable artifact of a normal memory system, **not** evidence the danger has passed. Decide from your contemporaneous record — notes, messages, photos, a friend's account — not from how it feels this week.
**Could also be:** a relationship that genuinely had good in it. Both things are usually true at once, and the warm memories are not fake. → **SAFETY:** [[dv-safety-planning]] · [[no-contact-exit-planning]]

### "Everything I read confirms what they told me"
**Candidates:** [[confirmation-bias]] · [[information-asymmetry]] · [[cult-conversion-funnel]] (the "do your own research" funnel, where the search terms were chosen for you)
**Could also be:** *warranted conviction* — sometimes the evidence really does point one way, and firm belief proportioned to strong evidence is not a bias. The tell is whether you can name what would change your mind, and whether the sources are independent of each other. → [[verification-rituals]] · [[epistemic-guardrails]]

### "I keep hearing this everywhere, so it must be true"
**Candidates:** [[fluency-familiarity]] (illusory truth — repetition makes a claim feel true regardless of whether it is) · [[disinformation-playbooks]] · [[propaganda-devices]] · [[overton-window-shifting]]
**Could also be:** *informed preference* — repeated **true** information is also repeated, and familiarity is often a valid signal. The tell is never repetition itself; it is repetition from a single interested source, or many sources tracing back to one. → [[inoculation-prebunking]] · [[verification-rituals]]

### "I'd never fall for that / I'm a good judge of character"
**Candidates:** [[self-evaluation-illusions]] · [[optimism-overconfidence]] · [[flattery-ego]] (the "you're too smart to be scammed" hook, which converts your competence into the reason not to check)
**Could also be:** *justified confidence* — competence is real, and about half of people genuinely are above the median at any given thing. The exposure isn't being confident; it's confidence in a domain where you have no scored track record. → [[verification-rituals]] · [[why-smart-people-fall]]

### "They keep telling me I'm overreacting to something that scares me"
**Candidates:** [[risk-misperception]] · [[gaslighting]] · [[tone-policing]] · [[coercive-control]]
**Read this first:** "that's just your normalcy bias" inverts the construct — normalcy bias names *under*-reaction, so aiming it at someone who is alarmed is a reality-denial move wearing a citation. Where you believe you are at risk, the record decides, not a bias label.
**Could also be:** *prudent risk aversion* misread by others, or a genuine over-estimate on your part — both happen. Neither is settled by whoever is most insistent. → **SAFETY:** [[dv-safety-planning]] · [[documentation-practices]]

### "One story convinced me / it just fits too well to be coincidence"
**Candidates:** [[probability-blindspots]] (a vivid case standing in for a rate) · [[pattern-illusions]] (structure seen in randomness) · [[cold-reading]] · [[ponzi-psychology]]
**Could also be:** *genuine pattern recognition* — trained perception detects real regularities, and real streaks and correlations exist. The tell is whether anyone can show you the denominator, and the misses as well as the hits. → [[verification-rituals]] · [[detection-heuristics]]

### "Questioning my church or my faith gets treated as a sin, not a question"
**Candidates:** [[spiritual-abuse]] · [[thought-terminating-cliches]] ("doubt your doubts") · [[shame-leverage]] · [[isolation-tactics]] (shunning, or the threat of it)
**Could also be:** *legitimate religion* — sincere faith, intense devotion and tight community are not abuse, and unusual beliefs are not a red flag. The markers are deception, exploitation, and **punished exit** — whether you can leave with your relationships, resources and selfhood intact. → [[cult-exit-support]] · [[boundary-scripts]]

### "Switching feels dangerous — they've made every alternative sound like a disaster"
**Candidates:** [[fud-competitor-tactics]] · [[fear-exploitation]] · [[manufactured-urgency]] · [[risk-misperception]] (zero-risk framing, "can you afford to take that chance?")
**Could also be:** a *genuine warning* — sometimes a switch really is risky, and an incumbent who names real risks is doing their job. The tell is who benefits from your fear, whether the risks come with rates, and whether they welcome you checking independently. → [[verification-rituals]] · [[detection-heuristics]]

### "They say they know what I'm really thinking / 'you know what you did'"
**Candidates:** [[mind-reading-illusions]] · [[gaslighting]] · [[emotional-blackmail]] · [[therapy-speak-weaponization]]
**Could also be:** *genuine perceptiveness* — long intimacy does produce real insight, and people who know you well are often right. The tell is unfalsifiability: a claim about your inner state that no denial can touch, deployed to win rather than to understand. → [[jade-avoidance]] · [[boundary-scripts]]

## The cardinal caveat

**Recognizing a feeling here does not diagnose the other person or prove manipulation.** Every candidate links to a *Caveats & false positives* section for a reason: distress is real but ambiguous, and seeing manipulation everywhere is its own harm ([[epistemic-guardrails]] on concept creep and base rates). Use this index to **generate hypotheses to check**, then verify against behavior over time. Label patterns of behavior — never diagnose a person.

## Safety notes

If you feel afraid, trapped, monitored, or in danger — that is the priority, ahead of naming the tactic:

- **US National DV Hotline 1-800-799-7233** (text START to 88788) · **988** Suicide & Crisis Lifeline · **911** if in immediate danger.
- **UK:** Refuge 0808 2000 247.
- See [[dv-safety-planning]] before changing anything an abuser could notice. Leaving is the highest-risk moment; plan it with an advocate.
