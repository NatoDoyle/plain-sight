# Glossary

One-line definitions and alias routing. Terms that have their own file point to it; keep definitions here to a single line. Maintained incrementally as topics complete.

## Terms

- **manipulation** → [[manipulation-vs-influence]] — covert influence that exploits psychological vulnerabilities rather than engaging reasons, typically benefiting the influencer at the target's expense
- **persuasion** → [[manipulation-vs-influence]] — transparent influence that engages a person's capacity for deliberation; survives full disclosure of its methods
- **coercion** → [[manipulation-vs-influence]] — influence through overt threat or force; removes acceptable alternatives rather than steering choice covertly
- **deception** — intentionally causing a false belief (by commission, omission, paltering, or equivocation); see [[lying-typologies]]
- **influence** — the umbrella term: any process that changes another's beliefs, feelings, or behavior; spans persuasion → manipulation → coercion
- **undue influence** → [[manipulation-vs-influence]] — legal doctrine: excessive persuasion overcoming a vulnerable person's free will, producing an inequitable result
- **coercive control** → [[coercive-control]] — a pattern of domination via micro-regulation, isolation, surveillance, and credible threat (criminalized in UK/Scotland and elsewhere)
- **concept creep** → [[epistemic-guardrails]] — the progressive semantic expansion of harm-related concepts (Haslam) to ever-milder or new phenomena
- **base-rate neglect** → [[epistemic-guardrails]] — ignoring prior probabilities when judging from vivid individual cues; the engine of manipulation over-detection
- **intimate terrorism** → [[epistemic-guardrails]] — Johnson's term for coercive-control-based partner violence (agency samples), vs **situational couple violence** (symmetric conflict escalation, community samples)
- **DARVO** → [[darvo]] — Deny, Attack, Reverse Victim and Offender: a perpetrator response pattern to being held accountable (Freyd)
- **dark patterns / deceptive patterns** → [[dark-patterns-obstruction]], [[dark-patterns-social-urgency]] — interface designs that deceive, obstruct, or pressure users into unintended choices
- **gaslighting** → [[gaslighting]] — sustained reality-denial that erodes a target's trust in their own perception, memory, or sanity
- **reciprocity** → [[reciprocity]] — the obligation ledger: unrequested favors create debts that can be called in disproportionately
- **commitment & consistency** → [[commitment-consistency]] — small yeses compound into identity-binding ladders; includes sunk-cost exploitation
- **social proof** → [[social-proof]] — the crowd as evidence; manufactured consensus steers judgment
- **authority** → [[authority]] — deference keys on fakeable signals (titles, uniforms, jargon), not verified competence
- **liking & similarity** → [[liking-similarity]] — we say yes to people we like and who feel like us; both are manufacturable
- **halo effect** → [[liking-similarity]] — one positive trait (beauty, fame, charm) spilling into unrelated judgments; also powers [[authority]]'s domain transfer
- **sunk cost** → [[commitment-consistency]] — continuing to justify past investment; the engine of escalation traps and recovery scams
- **scarcity & urgency** → [[scarcity-urgency]] — running-out value inflation + deadline pressure; manufactured to preempt deliberation
- **unity / in-group identity** → [[unity-ingroup]] — the manufactured "we": forced teaming, affinity exploitation, instant family
- **fear exploitation** → [[fear-exploitation]] — threat and rescue from the same hand; fear-then-relief compliance windows
- **guilt leverage** → [[guilt-leverage]] — unwarranted debts kept unpayable; FOG; the family ledger weaponized
- **shame leverage** → [[shame-leverage]] — worthlessness verdicts + exposure control; the silencing lever (sextortion, humiliation rituals)
- **hope & greed** → [[hope-greed]] — phantom fixation: disproportionate rewards that fund their own pursuit
- **flattery & ego** → [[flattery-ego]] — calibrated praise and special-status hooks; works even when seen through
- **attachment & belonging** → [[attachment-needs]] — the need to belong as attack surface: supply it fast, make it exclusive, price it
- **certainty needs** → [[certainty-needs]] — need for closure + the confidence heuristic; manufactured certainty sold into uncertainty
- **trust mechanics** → [[trust-mechanics]] — ability/benevolence/integrity cues, all fakeable; swift trust and its exploitation
- **intermittent reinforcement** → [[intermittent-reinforcement]] — variable rewards: maximal effort, maximal persistence, extinction bursts
- **emotional flooding** → [[emotional-flooding]] — arousal past the deliberation threshold; decisions harvested inside the state
- **cognitive overload** → [[cognitive-overload-confusion]] — flooded channels fail open: disrupt-then-reframe, sludge, jargon walls
- **information asymmetry** → [[information-asymmetry]] — engineered ignorance: credence goods, gatekeeping, milieu control
- **framing & anchoring** → [[framing-anchoring]] — reference-point control: first numbers, fake "was" prices, decoys
- **loss aversion & endowment** → [[loss-aversion-endowment]] — manufacture ownership, then price the taking-away
- **dissonance exploitation** → [[dissonance-exploitation]] — implicate the target; self-justification locks the door
- **memory fallibility** → [[memory-fallibility]] — reconstructive memory as gaslighting's substrate; first tellings are best evidence

## Contrast concepts

Innocent or neighboring look-alikes that the KB distinguishes manipulation **from**. These are registered link targets for `distinguished-from` frontmatter edges (parsed by `tools/kb.py`); they do not get their own files.

- `healthy-persuasion` — open appeal to reasons, evidence, or genuine shared interest; survives full disclosure; accepts "no"
- `honest-disagreement` — two parties sincerely holding different views or recollections without either steering the other covertly
- `memory-divergence` — two sincere people honestly remembering the same event differently (normal memory fallibility, not reality-denial)
- `assertiveness` — direct statement of one's own needs/limits; often mislabeled manipulation by those it inconveniences
- `boundary-setting` — declining, limiting contact, or protecting one's time/resources; self-protection, not punishment
- `hard-bargaining` — aggressive but disclosed negotiation positioning within recognized norms (anchoring high, slow concessions)
- `puffery` — legally tolerated subjective sales exaggeration no reasonable person relies on ("world's best coffee")
- `benign-concealment` — concealment without exploitation (surprise parties, privacy, tact); shows covertness alone ≠ manipulation
- `limerence` — naturally intense early-stage mutual infatuation; distinguishes from calculated [[love-bombing]]
- `incompetence-not-malice` — disorganization, forgetfulness, or poor skill producing harm without design (Hanlon's razor, within limits)
- `neurodivergent-communication` — ADHD forgetfulness, autistic directness/flat affect, etc., misread as tactics when no exploitation pattern exists
- `self-protective-distance` — withdrawal to regulate or stay safe (including by abuse victims), distinct from punitive [[silent-treatment]]
- `paternalism` — overriding someone's choices *for their benefit and openly* — ethically contested but distinct from self-serving covert steering
- `cultural-difference` — directness norms, haggling customs, gift obligations, or high-context communication misread as tactics across cultures
- `genuine-generosity` — giving with no calibration to an ask, no ledger speech, and no resentment when unrepaid; survives refusal
- `principled-consistency` — loyalty to values and evidence under pressure, including the freedom to change one's mind; distinct from compliance with extracted past commitments
- `rational-herding` — following the crowd under genuine uncertainty as sound Bayesian strategy (information cascades, honest review averages)
- `legitimate-expertise` — verifiable, in-domain authority that welcomes verification and second opinions
- `genuine-rapport` — warmth and common ground built at the pace of real acquaintance, symmetric in disclosure, surviving your first "no"
- `genuine-deadline` — a real, inelastic constraint honestly disclosed (statute dates, auction closes, finite stock); urgency is then information, not a lever
- `genuine-community` — belonging with real shared fate: freely exitable, identity not invoked to extract, leavers treated decently
- `genuine-warning` — a real threat honestly conveyed, where the warner does not sell the relief and welcomes independent verification
- `honest-hurt` — genuinely expressing pain at another's behavior without engineered obligation; discharges when addressed instead of compounding
- `earned-praise` — accurate, proportionate recognition with no ask attached; survives your failure to reciprocate
- `genuine-opportunity` — a real prospect that tolerates scrutiny, diversification, and delay; returns claimed are proportionate and verifiable
- `justified-confidence` — confidence proportional to evidence, with a visible track record and welcomed challenge; hedges where the domain demands it
- `self-protection` — a victim's defensive actions and accurate naming of harm done to them; routinely mislabeled as aggression or "DARVO" by the actual aggressor
- `genuine-victimhood` — actual harm accurately named; statistically the overwhelming majority of harm claims (false-report base rates run ~2–10%), and disbelief is the default injury such victims face
- `evolving-requirements` — standards that honestly change with circumstances: announced, symmetric, with the change acknowledged and prior work credited
- `genuine-reconciliation` — a sincere, behavior-backed attempt to repair and change after a breakup or rupture: takes responsibility, accepts "no," respects boundaries, and shows changed conduct over time rather than a flood of words
- `honest-feedback` — accurate, useful critique delivered to help the recipient improve; survives being asked "what specifically, and how do I fix it?" and is not timed to lower the recipient's standing
- `playful-teasing` — mutual, affectionate banter between equals that both enjoy and either can stop; distinct from status-lowering put-downs aimed one direction
- `genuine-dilemma` — a real hard choice with actual competing costs in the world, not engineered by a person who punishes every option; the tradeoff is in reality, not manufactured to trap
- `honest-reinterpretation` — sincerely understanding a past event differently over time (growth, new information, therapy) without asserting the new view is what everyone always agreed; non-deceptive, owns itself as a changed perspective
- `genuine-aspiration` — sincerely shared hopes, plans, and future-talk the speaker actually intends and takes real near-term steps toward; not used to extract present payment, and not contradicted by present conduct
- `genuine-crisis` — a real mental-health emergency (genuine suicidal distress, real fear) requiring compassionate, professional response; must NEVER be dismissed as manipulation, and can coexist with coercive deployment — respond to the safety risk first, regardless of suspected motive
- `prosocial-lie` — a lie told to benefit or spare the recipient (tact, "dinner was lovely", protecting a surprise), low-stakes and by convention; can raise warmth-trust even as it carries an integrity cost — not malicious deception
- `genuine-calibration` — mutual, refusal-respecting learning of each other's limits early in a relationship; bidirectional and non-escalating, distinct from one-directional probing that maps compliance for exploitation
- `false-self` — Winnicott's *unconscious, defensive* adaptation: a protective facade developed to comply with a caregiver's impingements; protects a vulnerable true self rather than extracting from a target — the opposite of strategic persona-fabrication
- `genuine-charisma` — authentic warmth, likeability, and social skill that is the person's real personality, not calibrated to disarm a specific target's scrutiny or timed to an extraction; survives knowing them over time
- `genuine-apology` — a sincere apology that *acknowledges responsibility* (the most load-bearing component), even if imperfect or missing other elements; aims at repair, not at defusing accountability — the opposite of a fauxpology
- `genuine-inquiry` — sincere questions or disagreement aimed at understanding or truth, responsive to answers and willing to update; must not be dismissed as "sealioning" or "bad faith" merely for being persistent or inconvenient
- `normal-relationship-conflict` — ordinary disagreement, friction, and rupture-then-genuine-repair between equals, without fear, a power-over campaign, or escalating control; the healthy baseline distinguished from the [[abuse-cycle]] and [[coercive-control]]
- `situational-couple-violence` — Johnson's conflict-driven violence that erupts from specific arguments without an underlying campaign of domination (more gender-symmetric; the dominant type in community samples); distinguished from coercive-control-based "intimate terrorism"
- `stockholm-syndrome` — the contested, weakly-evidenced popular construct of a reciprocal captor-captive bond (not a DSM diagnosis; seen in ~5–8% of hostages; coined after the 1973 Norrmalmstorg robbery and arguably used to discredit a victim); distinguished from the better-grounded, one-directional [[trauma-bonding]]
- `shared-hardship-bonding` — the ordinary, healthy closeness two people build by going through adversity together (a grueling job, grief, deployment); the widespread social-media MISUSE of "trauma bonding," which actually denotes attachment to an abuser — not the same thing
- `ordinary-mentorship` — genuine caring relationships between adults and children or mentees (teachers, coaches, relatives, clergy): warmth, attention, encouragement, even gifts, with no boundary-erosion, engineered isolation, secrecy, or sexual intent; overwhelmingly the norm, and distinguished from the [[grooming-sequence]]
- `legitimate-small-business` — genuine self-employment or direct sales where income comes mainly from real retail sales to outside customers (not recruitment or self-purchase), startup costs are transparent, and a meaningful share of participants can and do profit; the honest contrast to the [[mlm-lifecycle]]
- `illegal-pyramid-scheme` — a recruitment-driven structure where rewards flow primarily from enrolling others rather than selling product to real outside customers (Koscot/Omnitrition line); the neighboring construct an [[mlm-lifecycle]] insists it is *not*, though many blur into it
- `genuine-online-relationship` — a sincere remote relationship: the person will video-call and eventually meet, their identity is verifiable, disclosure is mutual, and it never pivots to money or investment asks; the innocent twin distinguished from the [[romance-scam-arc]]
- `legitimate-religion` — sincere faith, intense devotion, unusual beliefs, and tight community that use no deception in recruitment, permit members to leave with relationships/resources/selfhood intact, and don't exploit followers for a leader's benefit; intensity and strangeness are NOT what distinguish a high-control group ([[cult-conversion-funnel]]) — deception, exploitation, and punished exit are
- `legitimate-activism` — lawful political dissent, protest, advocacy, and even radical *opinions* held and expressed nonviolently; protected, normal, and NOT a pre-terrorist condition — distinguished from the [[radicalization-pipeline]], which is about mobilization to violence, not strength of belief
- `genuine-commitment` — freely-chosen deepening investment in a relationship, career, or cause where the costs of leaving are real, foreseeable, and disclosed, and exit remains genuinely possible; loyalty, perseverance, and grit are not entrapment — distinguished from [[escalation-entrapment]], where exit costs are *deliberately manufactured* to remove the option
- `shared-financial-management` — one partner handling household money by mutual, transparent agreement, with both retaining knowledge, access, and autonomy; a stay-at-home division of labor or pooled finances chosen freely — distinguished from [[financial-abuse]], which coercively restricts economic freedom
- `consensual-monitoring` — mutually-agreed location-sharing between partners, lawful parental monitoring of minor children, or disclosed employer monitoring of company devices; transparent and consented-to — distinguished from [[stalkerware-monitoring]], which is covert, non-consensual surveillance for control
- `sexual-negotiation` — mutual, pressure-free communication of sexual desire: initiating, flirting, asking, expressing disappointment once, and accepting a "no"; ordinary intimacy that respects autonomy — distinguished from [[sexual-coercion]], which applies pressure that overrides a "no" or erodes free choice
- `age-appropriate-responsibility` — chores, sibling help, helping during a genuine family crisis, and culturally-normative responsibility that is developmentally appropriate, fair/reciprocal, acknowledged, and time-limited; builds competence and is not [[parentification]] (which is chronic, age-inappropriate, unacknowledged role reversal serving the parent)
- `parental-differential-treatment` — ordinary, near-universal favoritism: treating children somewhat differently by need, temperament, or birth order, with a "favorite" being common and not inherently abusive; distinguished from [[golden-child-scapegoat]], where roles are rigid, unfair, and weaponized (the scapegoat blamed, the golden child used as a comparison tool)
- `loving-discipline` — setting expectations, enforcing consequences, and disapproving of a child's *behavior* while keeping love and regard for the *person* constant; healthy parenting (and the Rogerian/autonomy-support contrast) distinguished from [[conditional-love]], which withdraws affection or the relationship itself as leverage
- `legitimate-caregiving` — good-faith care, financial help, holding power of attorney, or being a beneficiary for an older relative; normal, common, and benign — distinguished from [[elder-targeting]], which exploits dependency and cognitive vulnerability (isolation + undue influence + an inequitable result)
- `collaborative-credit` — shared credit, building on others' ideas, a manager legitimately representing or synthesizing team work to leadership, and independent simultaneous discovery; normal and good — distinguished from [[credit-theft-visibility]], which systematically appropriates specific contributions and erases others' visibility for self-advancement
- `legitimate-performance-management` — a good-faith performance process: a real performance gap, achievable goals, genuine support/coaching, and intent to retain the employee; distinguished from [[pip-weaponization]], a process designed to fail with a predetermined exit
- `legitimate-collective-feedback` — multiple colleagues independently raising genuine, fair concerns about real conduct or performance, or ordinary team conflict; distinguished from [[mobbing]], which is coordinated, persistent hostility aimed at degrading and expelling a target who can't defend themselves
- `genuine-workplace-camaraderie` — real warmth, care, support, and close-knit teams that do not override fair pay, reasonable hours, boundaries, or the right to leave; good culture, distinguished from [[we-are-family-rhetoric]], which weaponizes loyalty/guilt to extract sacrifice
- `good-faith-ux` — honest, clear interface design that helps users decide, including necessary confirmation steps and security friction (2FA, "are you sure?"); distinguished from dark patterns ([[dark-patterns-obstruction]], [[dark-patterns-social-urgency]]) by *symmetry and honesty* — easy in is matched by easy out, and urgency/scarcity/social-proof signals are true
- `genuine-reviews` — authentic, unincentivized customer reviews and real grassroots opinion (even disclosed-incentivized reviews can be legitimate); distinguished from [[fake-reviews-astroturfing]], where the violation is fakery, undisclosed material connection, or sentiment-conditioned payment — not enthusiasm or organic coordination
- `genuine-parasocial-bond` — the normal, healthy one-sided affinity audiences feel for creators, performers, or public figures (enjoying their content, feeling you "know" them); distinguished from [[parasocial-influencer-tactics]], which covertly monetizes that bond via undisclosed selling — the bond is fine, the hidden commercial exploitation is the tactic
- `legitimate-ai-use` — disclosed, consensual uses of AI (assistants, accessibility text-to-speech voices, dubbing, clearly labeled synthetic media in entertainment); distinguished from [[ai-enabled-manipulation]], where the violation is *covert impersonation or deception about identity/authenticity* — not the use of AI itself. (Also the corrective to the "liar's dividend": "it's AI" is sometimes a false accusation against genuine media.)
- `genuine-perceptiveness` — real insight into a person from observation, expertise, empathy, or shared history (a good therapist, a perceptive friend), and openly-disclosed entertainment mentalism that doesn't claim a real power; distinguished from [[cold-reading]], where the violation is *claiming a special/supernatural power or secret knowledge for what is actually technique* — especially to extract money or control. Accurately reading a person is not fraud; faking a power to do so is.
- `healthy-self-esteem` — secure, realistic self-regard and ordinary confidence, pride, or ambition that doesn't require others' subjugation, can empathize, and tolerates criticism; the normal, adaptive end of the spectrum distinguished from [[narcissistic-patterns]] (and "healthy narcissism" in the clinical sense). High self-esteem, success, or even vanity is not a personality disorder.
- `healthy-strategic-thinking` — honest foresight, planning, negotiation, diplomacy, alliance-building, and self-interested strategy that stays truthful and non-exploitative; prudence and social skill are normal and adaptive, distinguished from [[machiavellianism]], which measures the cynical, deceptive, exploitative variant. Being strategic is not being Machiavellian.
- `ordinary-obliviousness` — normal not-noticing from missing information, distraction, reasonable trust, or plain mistakes; the base-rate explanation for "they didn't see it," distinguished from [[betrayal-blindness]], which is specifically the *motivated*, dependence-driven unawareness of a real betrayal by someone you rely on. Not every overlooked red flag is betrayal blindness.
- `normal-life-stress` — the ordinary stress and transitions everyone moves through (a hard week, a job change, normal grief) and navigates without being victimized; distinguished from [[crisis-windows]], which describes the *temporarily elevated exposure* of an acute transition — a state of reduced bandwidth and raised need, never a weakness, and never proof that manipulation is occurring.
- `healthy-solitude` — being alone by choice and content with it, and ordinary introversion; not a vulnerability and not something to fix. Distinguished from [[loneliness-isolation]], where the risk is *distressing unmet connection-need* or *imposed* isolation that removes the outside reality-check — not the enjoyment of one's own company.
- `healthy-agreeableness` — chosen, reciprocal, boundaried kindness, generosity, empathy, diplomacy, and culturally-normative accommodation that *retains the ability to say no*; normal and good. Distinguished from [[people-pleasing-fawn]], where accommodation is *fear-driven, compulsive self-erasure with no felt freedom to refuse*. Being kind is not a trauma response; the line is freedom, not niceness.
- `normal-cognitive-aging` — ordinary aging, including mild slowing, in a cognitively healthy older adult; the large majority of older people, who are no more gullible by virtue of age. Distinguished from [[cognitive-decline-age]], where the risk factor is *specific* decline (MCI, dementia, eroding financial reasoning) — not age itself. "Older = gullible" is a false and harmful stereotype.
