# Playbooks Compendium

> Manipulation rarely arrives as a single move. It runs as a **sequence** — a playbook — where each stage sets up the next and the early stages look benign *by design*. This file catalogs the named sequences across every domain, each with its **stage chain, signature tell, earliest exit point, and counter.** The deepest practical insight: **the cheapest place to get out is always the earliest stage** — before isolation, before dependence, before the exit costs are built. Recognizing the *shape* lets you locate where you are and jump out upstream.

## The universal arc

Most playbooks below are variations on one deep structure (read defensively — this is what to watch *being done to you*):

1. **Target selection** — find someone reachable: in a [[crisis-windows|crisis]], [[loneliness-isolation|lonely]], [[trauma-history-revictimization|previously victimized]], [[optimism-overconfidence|overconfident]], or simply trusting ([[why-smart-people-fall]]).
2. **Trust / rapport** — fast warmth, [[mirroring-false-identity|mirroring]], [[love-bombing]], [[flattery-ego]], shared identity ([[unity-ingroup]], [[affinity-fraud]]).
3. **Isolation / dependence** — become the central relationship; weaken outside checks ([[isolation-tactics]], [[financial-abuse]], cutting the [[betrayal-blindness|outside perspective]]).
4. **Exploitation** — extract the money, labor, compliance, or control the whole thing was for.
5. **Entrapment** — raise the cost of leaving until staying feels like the only option ([[escalation-entrapment]], [[trauma-bonding]], [[commitment-consistency|sunk cost]], shame).
6. **Disposal or recycle** — discard, or pull back in when you try to leave ([[hoovering]], [[breadcrumbing]]).

Not every playbook uses every stage, and **having stages is not itself proof of manipulation** — real relationships, real sales, and real communities also unfold over time (see the closing caveat). A playbook is defined by **deception + exploitation + engineered exit costs**, not by having an arc.

## Intimate & family

**Narcissistic / abusive relationship arc** — [[intimate-relationships]]
Sequence: [[love-bombing]] → devaluation ([[negging]], [[silent-treatment]], [[gaslighting]]) → discard → [[hoovering]], cycling on [[intermittent-reinforcement]].
Signature tell: the warmth is *conditional and cyclical* — idealize, devalue, repeat.
Earliest exit: the love-bombing stage — pressure to commit fast that resists slowing down.
Counter: [[detection-heuristics]] · [[no-contact-exit-planning]] · [[gray-rock]]

**The abuse cycle** — [[abuse-cycle]] (tension-building → incident → reconciliation/"honeymoon" → calm)
Signature tell: real remorse never converts to changed behavior; the calm is a stage, not a repair.
Earliest exit: name the *cycle* (not the single incident) — [[documentation-practices]] reveals the pattern. → [[dv-safety-planning]]

**Coercive control** — [[coercive-control]] (the standing system: [[isolation-tactics]] + [[financial-abuse]] + monitoring + micro-rules + [[veiled-threats]])
Signature tell: you're "walking on eggshells"; freedom has quietly shrunk.
Earliest exit: before isolation completes, while outside relationships survive. → [[dv-safety-planning]] (SAFETY)

**Financial-abuse entrapment** — [[financial-abuse]] + [[isolation-tactics]] → [[coercive-control]]
Signature tell: you have no independent access to money or information — leaving looks financially impossible.
Earliest exit: keep independent funds/accounts and documents *before* they're controlled. → [[dv-safety-planning]]

**DARVO (when confronted)** — [[darvo]] (Deny → Attack → Reverse Victim & Offender)
Signature tell: you raised a real grievance and within minutes you're the one apologizing.
Counter: hold the original issue; don't chase the reversal ([[jade-avoidance]]).

**Smear / "flying monkeys" campaign** — [[smear-campaigns]] + [[proxy-recruitment]] + [[triangulation]] (+ pre-emptive [[darvo]])
Signature tell: your reputation is being shaped before you can speak; others arrive already convinced.
Counter: [[documentation-practices]] · don't litigate via proxies.

## Predatory

**Grooming sequence** — [[grooming-sequence]] (target selection → trust/access → desensitization → isolation → abuse → secrecy/maintenance)
Signature tell: progressive [[boundary-testing|boundary erosion]] plus engineered one-on-one access and secrecy.
Earliest exit: the access/secrecy stage — refuse the "our little secret" frame; involve other adults. SAFETY · [[helping-others]]

## Scams & fraud

**The confidence game (long con)** — [[con-anatomy]] (put-up → play → rope → tale → convincer → the breakdown → send → touch → blow-off → fix)
Signature tell: an early "convincer" win; pressure to bring more money right after it.
Earliest exit: the rope/tale stage — independent verification kills most cons. → [[verification-rituals]]

**Romance scam / pig-butchering** — [[romance-scam-arc]] (contact → fast intimacy [[love-bombing]] → [[future-faking]] → introduce the "opportunity" → small withdrawal "convincer" → escalating deposits → the wall)
Signature tell: never video-meets; love and an investment opportunity arrive together.
Earliest exit: before the first deposit — reverse-image-search, refuse off-platform money. → [[verification-rituals]]

**Affinity-fraud / Ponzi** — [[affinity-fraud]] + [[ponzi-psychology]] (trusted in-group → too-good returns → [[social-proof]] from early "winners" → redemption theater → collapse)
Signature tell: the pitch leans on *who* vouches for it, not *how* it works; trouble withdrawing.
Earliest exit: independent due diligence regardless of who's recommending. → [[verification-rituals]]

**Phishing / BEC (business email compromise)** — [[phishing-pretexting]] (pretext → spoofed [[authority]] + [[manufactured-urgency]] → request for credentials or a wire/bank-detail change)
Signature tell: urgent payment or login request that discourages the normal check.
Earliest exit: out-of-band verification on a *known* number, every time. → [[organizational-defenses]] · [[verification-rituals]]

## Sales & negotiation

**The close stack** — [[sales]] ([[foot-in-the-door]] → [[commitment-consistency]] → [[thats-not-all-decoys]] / [[door-in-the-face]] → [[manufactured-urgency]] → close)
Signature tell: a cascade of small yeses funneling to a now-or-never.
Counter: "I don't decide today" defeats most of it ([[detection-heuristics]]).

**Four-square car deal** — [[four-square-payment-packing]] + [[lowballing]] + [[bad-faith-negotiation]] (higher authority, the nibble)
Signature tell: the conversation moves to monthly payment, and a "manager" must approve.
Counter: negotiate out-the-door price only; bring financing; walk away is your power. → [[bad-faith-negotiation]]

**Timeshare / high-pressure pitch** — [[timeshare-playbook]] (free-gift [[reciprocity]] → hours-long [[cognitive-overload-confusion]] → [[manufactured-urgency]] "today only" → liberation/"tour" close)
Signature tell: you can't leave the room and the price evaporates if you don't sign now.
Counter: leave; no legitimate deal requires signing before you can think. → [[verification-rituals]]

## Recruitment systems

**MLM lifecycle** — [[mlm-lifecycle]] + [[mlm-recruitment-scripts]] + [[affinity-fraud]] (recruit via warm market → hope/dream-selling → buy inventory & training → recruit downline → most lose money → blame the victim)
Signature tell: income comes from recruiting, not product sales; upline blames *your* effort.
Earliest exit: ask for the income-disclosure statement before any purchase. → [[detection-heuristics]]

**Cult conversion funnel** — [[cult-conversion-funnel]] (low-pressure intro → [[love-bombing]] → escalating commitment → [[isolation-tactics]] → [[thought-terminating-cliches]] → dependence, punished exit)
Signature tell: information control, us-vs-them, and a rising cost of leaving.
Earliest exit: keep outside relationships and independent information alive. → [[cult-exit-support]]

**Radicalization pipeline** — [[radicalization-pipeline]] (grievance → community/belonging → us-vs-them → escalating norms → action)
Signature tell: a single narrative explains everything and dehumanizes an out-group.
Counter: [[inoculation-prebunking]] · maintain cross-cutting ties. → [[cult-exit-support]]

## How to use this compendium

1. **Find your arena** (intimate, sales, scam, work, online). 2. **Locate where you are in the sequence** — the stages before yours are how you got here; the stages after are what's coming. 3. **Jump out upstream** — the earliest-exit line is the highest-leverage move, because exit costs only grow. 4. Follow the linked **dynamic file** for the full Recognition/Counter detail, and the linked **defense** for the script.

## Caveat: arcs are not proof

Real love, real sales funnels, real religious conversion, and real political persuasion all unfold in stages too — *having an arc is not manipulation*. What marks a **playbook** is the combination the universal arc hides: **concealed intent, exploitation, and deliberately engineered exit costs** (see [[manipulation-vs-influence]] and the false-positive caveats in each linked file). Use these patterns to orient, then confirm against behavior — and never to diagnose a person ([[epistemic-guardrails]]).
